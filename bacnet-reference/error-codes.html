BACnet error, reject and abort codes

Updated 7 November 2026

Short answer

When a read or write fails, the device answers with an Error (class + code), a Reject or an Abort. Each is a number. Here is what every one means, with notes on the ones you will actually see. Examples: 2 = property.

Looking for a number but not sure which list it belongs to? Use the BACnet number lookup, which searches every table at once.

Values come from the open-source bacnet-stack 1.6.1 (bacenum.h), which follows ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 135, and were cross-checked one by one against two other independent BACnet stacks (bacpypes and @bacnet-js/client). Entries marked (newer) are in bacnet-stack only, because the other two stacks predate them. Numbers 0-255 (and the higher ASHRAE ranges) are standard; vendors may use proprietary numbers above them.
