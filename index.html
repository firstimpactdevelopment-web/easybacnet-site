Easy BACnet · BACnet IP Controls made Easy

Browse and control BACnet/IP devices, straight from your phone

What this is

Easy BACnet is a BACnet/IP browser and control tool for anyone who works with BACnet devices on a network — field technicians, systems integrators, controls engineers, and facilities and maintenance teams. Discover what is on the network, browse each device and its points, read live values, command a point at the priority you choose (and release it), export a full report, and build your own on-screen control panels for the equipment you touch most.

No account, no analytics, no server — everything happens on your phone and your local network.

What it does

  • Discover the network — finds every BACnet/IP device on the subnet, including devices reached through a BACnet router on an MS/TP trunk.
  • Browse devices and points — every object on every device, with its type, instance number, name, live present value, units, status flags and description. A full BACnet/IP browser in your pocket.
  • See who is commanding a point — the priority array and relinquish default, so you can tell at a glance whether a point is being overridden, and at what level.
  • Command and release — write a value at the BACnet priority you choose, then release it back to automatic when you are done. Write mode is off by default and turns itself off every time the app starts.
  • Build custom control panels — lay out your own screen for a unit with drag-and-drop setpoints, toggles, multi-state pickers and live readouts, so the handful of points you actually use are one tap away every visit.
  • Export a report — the whole system as a CSV: device ID, name and IP, object type and number, point name, present value, units and status — ready to hand to an integrator or keep on file.

Before you connect

  • Put the phone on the same subnet as the controllers. BACnet discovery relies on broadcast, and broadcast traffic does not cross a router without a BBMD — so a phone on guest Wi‑Fi or a separate VLAN will not see the equipment.
  • Discovery runs on UDP 47808, the standard BACnet/IP port. Some sites assign 47809 and above; the app checks the common range automatically.
  • Reading is safe. Writing carries real weight. A commanded point holds the value you write until it is released. If you intend to command anything on a live system, read how BACnet priority works first.
