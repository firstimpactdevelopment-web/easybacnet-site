Short answer

In water-to-water units the MPC controls water temperature instead of space temperature. The BMS commands occupancy, selects heating or cooling with the MC Switch, writes occupied and unoccupied water setpoints and differentials, and can shut the unit down or take direct control of the compressors and reversing valve. Control is on entering water temperature by default and can be moved to leaving water temperature. The unit stays in the selected mode until the mode is changed.

BMS integration notes: ClimateMaster MPC Multi-Protocol DDC Controller - Water-to-Water (Gen 8 and newer)

Integration in brief

Address the MPC with the rotary switches and set the MS/TP baud with the DIP switches. Command occupancy with BV 12, choose heating or cooling with BV 7 MC Switch, and write the occupied water setpoints. Use BV 8 for an emergency stop and BV 4 to clear a lockout. Monitor the compressor, load water temperatures and the Alarm State.

Enable & remote start

BV 12 Occupied Mode (default ON) sets occupied/unoccupied; BV 14 Work Schedule can take a BMS schedule instead. BV 7 MC Switch selects cooling (ON) or heating (OFF) and the unit stays in that mode until it is changed. BV 8 Emergency Shutdown turns the outputs off. With BV 62 Multi Stage Enable ON the BMS can drive C1, C2 and the reversing valve directly through BV 61, BV 63 and BV 60.

Sensors & values from the BMS

BV 9 CC Mode and BV 10 HC Mode move cooling and heating control from entering (OFF, default) to leaving (ON) water temperature. Follower units (BV 16 ON) take the leader's water temperature in Master WT (AV 4/AV 27) and its setpoints in AV 9/AV 10.

Network setup

  • MAC address comes from two rotary switches (00-99); ClimateMaster suggests 02 for heat pump 1 and HP number + 1 for the rest.
  • BACnet MS/TP on RS-485 at 9.6, 19.2, 38.4 or 76.8 kbps is set with DIP switches SW1-SW4; the Communications Selection jumper picks RS-485 or BACnet over ARC156 (156 kbps).
  • The device instance is auto-generated with the address as its last two digits; for more than 99 units turn off 'Auto Generate Device ID' in the Equipment Touch tool and enter it there.
  • The same port can run N2 or Modbus instead of BACnet; the protocol is chosen with the same switches.

Watch out for

  • The unit does not change between heating and cooling by itself; the BMS or a technician must switch BV 7.
  • Occupied defaults are 53 °F cooling and 105 °F heating; unoccupied defaults 73 / 85 °F.
  • System Reset (BV 4) is not momentary; write ON, then OFF.
  • Compressor and RV overrides only act while BV 62 Multi Stage Enable is ON.

Integration notes

  • Points list from the MPC manual's points matrix; the same table also gives Modbus registers and N2 IDs.
  • Temperatures follow BV 33 Metric (OFF = °F); some setpoints have separate °F and °C objects.
  • The source lists BV 21 twice (UPS Signal and Lockout Alarm); only Lockout Alarm is kept here, since it fits the BV 19-24 sequence. UPS Signal sits where water-to-air units have BV 15; find its real instance with a device discovery.
  • The fault counter descriptions refer to a Fault Count Reset BV 22 that is not in the water-to-water table.
  • BV 7, BV 9 and BV 10 have no access marked in the source; they are shown writable because the manual says the mode and control sensor are changed over BACnet. AV 34-36 have none marked and are shown read-only.

Which ClimateMaster MPC Multi-Protocol DDC Controller - Water-to-Water (Gen 8 and newer) BACnet points are writable?

42 of the 76 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.
