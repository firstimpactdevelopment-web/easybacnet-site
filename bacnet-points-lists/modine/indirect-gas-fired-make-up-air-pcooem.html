Short answer

Modine's indirect gas-fired heating and make-up air units use a Carel pCOOEM+ controller; with a BMS interface card it talks BACnet MS/TP or BACnet IP/Ethernet (LonWorks is the other option). The BMS can switch the unit on and off, send occupancy, reset alarms, send a heartbeat and write space, supply-air, neutral-air, dewpoint, humidity, CO2, damper and fan-speed setpoints. It reads temperatures, dewpoints, enthalpies, humidities, pressures, demands, digital inputs, relay outputs and a long list of individual alarm flags.

BMS integration notes: Modine Indirect Gas-Fired Heating & Make-Up Air, Series D/H/I/O (Modine Controls System, pCOOEM+)

Integration in brief

Choose BACnet MSTP or BACnet IP/Eth as the BMS-1 protocol in Service > BMS Config and enable the BACnet plug-in to set the instance and address. Use BMS_OnOff (BV 6) and BMS_Occupied (BV 7) to run the unit, write the occupied space heating setpoint (AV 15) or supply air setpoints, and reset alarms with BV 8. Monitor Unit_Status, supply air temperature, airflow proving and the general alarm BV 1.

Enable & remote start

The unit is first enabled from the display's ON/OFF UNIT menu; On/Off by BMS (BV 6) is one of the secondary sources, available when a BMS interface card is fitted. Occupancy can also come from the BMS (BV 7), a digital input, the clock schedule or the pAD override; occupied/unoccupied setpoints need a space pAD.

Heartbeat & communication loss

The BMS Config menu has Enable Heartbeat (default No), threshold and timeout settings and 'If BMS Offline Turn Unit' Off/On. The BMS writes BV 9 BMS_Heartbeat_In; when it stops, alarm A032 BMS Offline is raised.

Network setup

  • BMS-1 protocol: BACnet MSTP, BACnet IP/Eth or LON, set in Service > BMS Config; set BACnet PlugIn = Yes to edit the BACnet settings.
  • Device instance range 0-4194999 in the menu.
  • BACnet MS/TP: baud 9600-76800, MAC 0-127, Max Masters and Max Info Frames set in the menu.
  • BACnet IP: IP, subnet, gateway, DNS set in the menu; use Function = Write and Update = Yes to send them to the card.

Watch out for

  • Integer AVs start at 1001; do not confuse AV 1-51 (analog) with AV 1001+ (integer).
  • Occupied and unoccupied setpoints only apply when a space pAD is installed.
  • Furnace 2/3, evaporative cooler, DX/CW and A2L points only carry data when those options are fitted.

Integration notes

  • Carel analog variables are AV 1-51, Carel integer variables are AV 1001-1084 and digital variables are BV 1-81.

How many BACnet points does the Modine Indirect Gas-Fired Heating & Make-Up Air, Series D/H/I/O (Modine Controls System, pCOOEM+) have?

Which Modine Indirect Gas-Fired Heating & Make-Up Air, Series D/H/I/O (Modine Controls System, pCOOEM+) BACnet points are writable?

47 of the 175 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Modine Indirect Gas-Fired Heating & Make-Up Air, Series D/H/I/O (Modine Controls System, pCOOEM+)?

Start with BV 6 BMS_OnOff (BMS on/off command); BV 7 BMS_Occupied (BMS occupied command); AV 15 Space_Heat_SP_Occ_BMS (Occupied space heating setpoint); AV 20 SA_Heat_Res_SP_Occ_BMS (Occupied supply air heating setpoint); BV 9 BMS_Heartbeat_In (BMS heartbeat, when enabled in the BMS menu); BV 8 BMS_Alarm_Reset (Alarm reset command); AV 1001 Unit_Status (Unit status code); BV 12 Unit_On_Airflow_Okay (Fan enabled and airflow proven); AV 1 SA_Temp_BMS (Supply air temperature); BV 1 Alarm_On (General alarm).

How do I enable BMS control of the Modine Indirect Gas-Fired Heating & Make-Up Air, Series D/H/I/O (Modine Controls System, pCOOEM+)?

The unit is first enabled from the display's ON/OFF UNIT menu; On/Off by BMS (BV 6) is one of the secondary sources, available when a BMS interface card is fitted. Occupancy can also come from the BMS (BV 7), a digital input, the clock schedule or the pAD override; occupied/unoccupied setpoints need a space pAD.

Does the Modine Indirect Gas-Fired Heating & Make-Up Air, Series D/H/I/O (Modine Controls System, pCOOEM+) need a BACnet heartbeat or have a communication timeout?

The BMS Config menu has Enable Heartbeat (default No), threshold and timeout settings and 'If BMS Offline Turn Unit' Off/On. The BMS writes BV 9 BMS_Heartbeat_In; when it stops, alarm A032 BMS Offline is raised.
