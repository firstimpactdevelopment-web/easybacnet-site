Sierra Instruments InnovaMass 240i/241i Vortex Flow Meter BACnet points list

Updated 6 November 2026

Short answer

The InnovaMass 240i (inline) and 241i (insertion) are multivariable vortex meters measuring flow, temperature and pressure for steam, gas and liquids. Over BACnet MS/TP they expose 21 Analog Inputs (flow, temperatures, pressure, energy, totalizer, alarm state and vortex diagnostics), 11 Binary Input fault flags and a Binary Output to reset the totalizer.

This page lists 33 BACnet objects for the Sierra Instruments InnovaMass 240i/241i Vortex Flow Meter — 1 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from Sierra Instruments’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: Sierra Instruments InnovaMass 240i/241i Vortex Flow Meter

Integration in brief

Read flow, temperature, pressure, the totalizer and energy flow, and watch the BI fault flags and alarm AIs. Write the Reset Totalizer BO to clear the total.

Questions about the Sierra Instruments InnovaMass 240i/241i Vortex Flow Meter

How many BACnet points does the Sierra Instruments InnovaMass 240i/241i Vortex Flow Meter have?

This list has 33 BACnet objects: 21 analog inputs, 11 binary inputs, 1 binary output. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Sierra Instruments InnovaMass 240i/241i Vortex Flow Meter BACnet points are writable?

1 of the 33 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done. The source has no read/write column, so this is inferred from object type.

Where is the official Sierra Instruments InnovaMass 240i/241i Vortex Flow Meter BACnet documentation?

The source is InnovaMass 240i/241i Series BACnet Instruction Manual (IM240i/241i BACnet), revision V3.4/19, published free by Sierra Instruments at https://sierrainstruments.com/userfiles/file/manuals/240i-241i-bacnet-instruction-manual.pdf?x=5806.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Sierra Instruments InnovaMass 240i/241i Vortex Flow Meter?

Start with AI 1 Flow (Flow rate); AI 6 Totalizer (Flow total); AI 2 Temperature1 (Process temperature); AI 4 Pressure (Process pressure); AI 5 Energy Flow (Energy flow); AI 7 Alarm Status (Alarm relay state).
