Short answer

The Condair RO-A supplies reverse osmosis water to humidifiers. Through the Integrated Controller's BACnet on-board interface it reports operating and service status, capacity, maintenance and operating hours, inlet and tank pressures, pump overcurrent, leak sensor and valve states. All objects are read-only.

BMS integration notes: Condair Condair RO-A

Integration in brief

Read-only monitoring of the RO-A through the Integrated Controller's BACnet on-board interface: map status, service status, fault code, capacity and the leak sensor.

Network setup

  • Communication settings are in the Communication submenu (password 0335): BACnet = Off (factory), MSTP or BACnet/IP; some changes reboot the controller.
  • Node ID defaults to 1001 (range 1-4194303); BACnet IP port 47808; Foreign Device (BBMD) registration Off by default.
  • IP type defaults to DHCP and reverts to fixed addressing after 5 failed DHCP attempts.
  • MS/TP: Master by default, MAC 127 (0-127 master, 0-254 slave), baud 9600 (9600-76800).

Watch out for

  • All RO-A objects are read-only.
  • Pressure units follow the Metric/Imperial setting.

Questions about the Condair Condair RO-A

How many BACnet points does the Condair Condair RO-A have?

This list has 13 BACnet objects: 6 analog inputs, 4 binary inputs, 1 device, 2 multi-state inputs. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Condair Condair RO-A BACnet points are writable?

None of the listed objects are marked writable in the source documentation.

Where is the official Condair Condair RO-A BACnet documentation?

The source is BACnet on-board - Supplemental Installation and Operation Manual (RS, GS II, DL, ME and RO-A) (2595492-C), revision 2022-12, published free by Condair at https://www.condair.com/consense/bms-communication-ic-1.0/am_bms_interface_bacnet_on_board_2595492_en_condairltd.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Condair Condair RO-A?

Start with MI 731 Status RO (RO operating status); MI 549 Service Status A (Service/fault status); AI 10823 Fault Code (Active fault code); BI 727 Leakage RO (Leak sensor); AI 10825 Tank pressure RO (Pure water tank pressure); AI 10779 Capacity RO (RO capacity).

How do I check these points on the real Condair RO-A?
