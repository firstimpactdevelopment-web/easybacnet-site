Short answer

The JMWL-Series is a modular water-to-water heat pump (chiller/heater) from Johnson Controls. Each module's master controller exposes about 300 objects over BACnet MS/TP: physical inputs and outputs (AI, AO, BI, BO), status, setpoint and alarm values (AV, BV) and a few multistate values for model, refrigerant, valve options and unit mode. The BAS enables cooling or heating through two BAS command BVs and writes the leaving water setpoints.

BMS integration notes: Johnson Controls JMWL-Series 30-70 Ton Modular Water-to-Water Heat Pump

Integration in brief

Make sure the local cool and heat enable inputs are open, write the leaving water setpoint (cool_temp_stp_1, 42-62 °F, or heat_temp_stp_1, 80-135 °F), then set cool_enable_bas_1 (BV 52) or heat_enable_bas_1 (BV 53) on and the other off. The module opens its valves, proves load and source flow (BV 9, BV 73, BV 38) and starts the run_cool or run_heat sequence.

Enable & remote start

With the local dig_cool_enable_1 and dig_heat_enable_1 inputs open, BV 52 cool_enable_bas_1 or BV 53 heat_enable_bas_1 starts the module in cooling or heating; only one should be on.

Network setup

  • MS/TP baud is set with DIP switches 1 and 2 on the controller.
  • BACnet network numbers and device instances are set from the controller's FN(0) and FN(4) menus.

Watch out for

  • Physical outputs (BO, AO) are not marked read-only in the table; writing them overrides the module's own control.
