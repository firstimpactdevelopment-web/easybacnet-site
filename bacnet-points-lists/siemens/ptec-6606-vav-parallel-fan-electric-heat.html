Siemens BACnet PTEC VAV Controller - Application 6606 (Parallel Fan with Electric Heat) BACnet points list

Updated 28 October 2026

Short answer

Application 6606 controls a parallel fan-powered VAV box with up to three stages of electric heat on a Siemens BACnet PTEC controller over BACnet MS/TP. The supply damper modulates for cooling; in heating the parallel fan recirculates plenum air and the time-modulated heat stages follow the heating loop. The fan can be set up as the first stage of heat (PARALLEL ON/OFF against the heating loop) or to run only when supply airflow is too low for the coil (against FLOW).

BMS integration notes: Siemens BACnet PTEC VAV Controller - Application 6606 (Parallel Fan with Electric Heat)

Integration in brief

Address the controller on the BACnet MS/TP trunk (manually or with auto addressing), then release the box to application control. The BMS commands occupancy with DAY.NGT (BO 29) and the day setpoints, and reads room temperature, airflow, damper position, fan and heat stage status and the error status.

Enable & remote start

Occupancy is DAY.NGT (BO 29). With a wall switch on DI 2 and WALL SWITCH (BO 18) = YES, the switch sets it; standalone with no switch the controller stays in day mode; on a network the field panel or BMS commands DAY.NGT. NGT OVRD (BO 21) shows a temporary night override from the room unit button.

Sensors & values from the BMS

VENT DMD MIN (AO 105) sets the occupied ventilation minimum airflow and can be adjusted by the field panel; the larger of it and the cooling or heating minimum is used.

Network setup

  • BACnet MS/TP only. CTLR ADDRESS (AO 1) holds the MS/TP address; the factory value is 255.
  • APPLICATION (AO 2) holds the application number; the point table lists its factory default as 6687.

Watch out for

  • As shipped, the controller keeps all equipment OFF until it is released to application control (see Siemens start-up procedures).
  • With a digital room unit (revision 26 and later), a command sent through the room unit puts the matching BACnet input Out Of Service.
  • Loop gains, motor timing, rotation angles and setup codes are commissioning values; don't write them from the BMS routinely.
  • The fan-to-supplement-airflow setup is only safe when the heating coils sit in the discharge airflow; with coils in the return/plenum path it can damage the coils.

Integration notes

  • AI and BI objects are read-only. AO and BO objects are BACnet output objects with a priority array; the guide has no access column, so access is inferred from object type. Many AOs are calculated status values, not commands.
  • Instances shown in braces in the guide may be unbundled at a field panel; that marking is not reproduced here.
  • Units are given in US units (°F, cfm, ft²); the guide also lists SI factory defaults.
  • The guide covers controllers with firmware newer than revision Bx40; older firmware has separate documentation.
  • The series fan application 6604 (doc 140-1160) prints the same object list, names and instances; only the fan sequence differs.
  • The parallel fan is on DO 6 (BO 46); heat stages are on DO 3-DO 5 (BO 43-45).
