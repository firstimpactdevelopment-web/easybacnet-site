Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit BACnet points list

Updated 16 October 2026

Short answer

The CMU is a packaged make-up air heater with its own controller that exposes a fixed BACnet MS/TP object table. Objects are grouped into Integrator, Advanced and Factory lists, and MSV.2 Cfg_ObjectListMode selects which group the controller shows on the network. Points cover supply, room and outdoor air readings, airflow and temperature setpoints, damper and exhaust fan settings, and a long set of heater and sensor alarms.

This page lists 130 BACnet objects for the Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit — 76 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from Neptronic’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit

Integration in brief

Start/stop the unit with BV.18 SystemOnOff, choose where the temperature setpoint comes from with MSV.17 (set Network to use AV.10), and command the fan through AV.7 CurrentAirflowSetpoint. Monitor supply air temperature, occupancy state and the alarm BVs.

Enable & remote start

BV.18 SystemOnOff turns the system on or off from the network. Occupancy can come from inputs (BV.13 enables them, BV.17 picks Unoccupied or Off when inactive) or from the internal schedule enabled by BV.11; without a schedule the unit stays occupied.

Sensors & values from the BMS

Set MSV.17 Cfg_TempSetpointSource to Network and write AV.10 NetworkTempSetpoint. MSV.16 selects whether control is on supply (SAT) or room (RAT) temperature; the doc lists no BMS-written temperature input.

Network setup

  • BACnet MS/TP only; the device instance defaults to 153000 + MAC address (auto) and can be changed from the menu or by writing the Device Object_Identifier.
  • MAC address defaults to 0; baud rate auto-detects (9600/19200/38400/76800), or set it by writing Device proprietary property 1001.
  • Max_Master defaults to 127; lower it to the highest MAC on the trunk for faster token passing.
  • A Copy Config function in the menu can clone one controller's configuration to others of the same type on the trunk.

Watch out for

  • Set MSV.2 Cfg_ObjectListMode to Advanced or Factory to see the objects outside the Integrator list.
  • AV.7, AV.13 and a few outputs are commandable; write at a consistent priority and relinquish when done.
  • BV.20 and BV.35 are internal communication alarms between the CMU main board and its add-on board, not BMS heartbeats.
  • Out_of_Service on read-only objects is writable for testing, but it reverts automatically after 15 minutes (Device proprietary property 1002, 0-120 min, 0 = never).

Questions about the Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit

How many BACnet points does the Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit have?

This list has 130 BACnet objects: 14 analog inputs, 2 analog outputs, 48 analog values, 4 binary inputs, 2 binary outputs, 40 binary values, 20 multi-state values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit BACnet points are writable?

76 of the 130 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit BACnet documentation?

The source is Compact Make-up Air Unit BACnet Communication Module User Guide (CMU-BACnet Guide-211201), revision 2021-12-01, published free by Neptronic at https://www.neptronic.com/heaters/pdf/20211206/CMU-BACnet%20Guide-211201.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit?

Start with BV 18 SystemOnOff (Turns the unit on or off); MV 17 Cfg_TempSetpointSource (Select OnBoard, Tstat or Network setpoint source); AV 10 NetworkTempSetpoint (Temperature setpoint written by the BMS); AV 7 CurrentAirflowSetpoint (Fan speed / airflow setpoint % (commandable)); AV 1 SAT (Supply air temperature); MV 1 OccupancyState (Occupied, unoccupied or off); BV 4 CMUFanEnable (Shows whether the supply fan is enabled); BV 19 HostThermalCutoutAlarm (Heater thermal cutout alarm); BV 28 HostAirFlowDetectAlarm (Airflow sensor alarm).

How do I enable BMS control of the Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit?

BV.18 SystemOnOff turns the system on or off from the network. Occupancy can come from inputs (BV.13 enables them, BV.17 picks Unoccupied or Off when inactive) or from the internal schedule enabled by BV.11; without a schedule the unit stays occupied.

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the Neptronic CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit?

Set MSV.17 Cfg_TempSetpointSource to Network and write AV.10 NetworkTempSetpoint. MSV.16 selects whether control is on supply (SAT) or room (RAT) temperature; the doc lists no BMS-written temperature input.

How do I check these points on the real CMU Compact Make-up Air Unit?

Scan the network with a BACnet IP browser such as Easy BACnet, open the device and compare its object list with this table. Anything missing is usually an option that isn't installed or a point that is disabled in the controller's configuration.
