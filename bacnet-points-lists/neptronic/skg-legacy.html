Updated 12 November 2026

Short answer

The NEPICSKG module gives the older SKG gas-fired humidifier a BACnet MS/TP interface (B-ASC). A common block (instances 1-99) carries humidity output, demand, room and duct humidity, setpoints, delays, PID settings, status and alarm flags and the control, operation and source selectors; each tank adds its own block (101-199 for tank 1 up to 401-499 for tank 4) with water and chimney temperatures, water level, burner demand and fan speed, timers, status flags and alarms.

BMS integration notes: Neptronic SKG (legacy, pre-SKG4) gas-fired humidifier with NEPICSKG BACnet module

Integration in brief

For BMS demand control set MSV 1 Ctl_Mode to 3 and write AV 1 Humidity_Demand; for the internal PID set MSV 1 to 2, choose the setpoint and humidity sources (MSV 4-6) and write AV 16 and AV 2. MSV 2 turns the unit off, on or to drain. Read BI 1, AI 1 and the two summary alarms BI 4 and BI 5.

Enable & remote start

MSV 2 Operation_Mode sets the unit off (1), on (2) or to drain (3). MSV 1 Ctl_Mode picks the demand source: 1 = analog input 1, 2 = internal PID, 3 = remote demand over BACnet.

Sensors & values from the BMS

With MSV 5 Room_Hum_Source = 2 and MSV 6 Duct_Hum_Source = 3 (remote) the BMS writes AV 2 Room_Humidity and AV 4 Duct_Humidity; MSV 4 SP_Source = 3 takes the setpoint from BACnet.

Network setup

  • A configuration DIP switch puts the module in configure mode (MAC 254, wildcard device instance 4194303); two more DIP switches set 9600, 19200, 38400 or 76800 baud.
  • Defaults: MAC 0, device instance 153000, device name NEPSKGHumidifier153000, Max_Master 127; MAC and device instance can also be set from the unit keypad in configure mode.

Watch out for

  • Tank objects only respond for tanks that exist on the unit.
  • Temperatures follow BV 1 Temperature_Unit (Celsius or Fahrenheit).

Integration notes

  • Objects for tanks 2-4 (instances 201-499) exist only on units with that many tanks; the guide gives 95 objects for one tank up to 200 for four.
  • The tank 4 table prints OverFlow_DripPan4 as BV 414, the same instance as Tank_Temp_Low4 (it is probably BV 413); both rows are kept as printed.
  • Typos kept as printed: H3OLvl_Prb_Bad3 / H4OLvl_Prb_Bad4 and the space in "Modulation Stat1-4".
  • MSV 5 Room_Hum_Source is printed without the W flag the other selectors have.
  • Access is from the W? column; the tank alarm BVs are printed as writable. The module does not send BACnet event notifications.

Questions about the Neptronic SKG (legacy, pre-SKG4) gas-fired humidifier with NEPICSKG BACnet module

How many BACnet points does the Neptronic SKG (legacy, pre-SKG4) gas-fired humidifier with NEPICSKG BACnet module have?

This list has 199 BACnet objects: 37 analog inputs, 40 analog values, 42 binary inputs, 70 binary values, 4 multi-state inputs, 6 multi-state values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Neptronic SKG (legacy, pre-SKG4) gas-fired humidifier with NEPICSKG BACnet module BACnet points are writable?

115 of the 199 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Neptronic SKG (legacy, pre-SKG4) gas-fired humidifier with NEPICSKG BACnet module BACnet documentation?

The source is Gas Fired Humidifier SKG Series - BACnet Communication Module User Guide (SKG-BACNET), revision Rev 060803, published free by Neptronic at https://www.neptronic.com/Humidifiers/PDF/SKG3000/SKG-bacnet-user-guide.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Neptronic SKG (legacy, pre-SKG4) gas-fired humidifier with NEPICSKG BACnet module?

Start with MV 2 Operation_Mode (Unit off, on or drain); MV 1 Ctl_Mode (Control mode; 3 = demand from BACnet); AV 1 Humidity_Demand (Humidity demand from the BMS, %); AV 16 Ctl_SP (Humidity setpoint for internal PID, %RH); AV 2 Room_Humidity (Room RH from the BMS (remote source)); BI 1 Power_Status (Unit power on); AI 1 Humidity_Out (Humidifier output, %); BI 4 Alrm_Not_Critical (Non-critical alarm, unit still running); BI 5 Alarm_Critical (Critical alarm, unit stopped).

How do I enable BMS control of the Neptronic SKG (legacy, pre-SKG4) gas-fired humidifier with NEPICSKG BACnet module?

MSV 2 Operation_Mode sets the unit off (1), on (2) or to drain (3). MSV 1 Ctl_Mode picks the demand source: 1 = analog input 1, 2 = internal PID, 3 = remote demand over BACnet.

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the Neptronic SKG (legacy, pre-SKG4) gas-fired humidifier with NEPICSKG BACnet module?

With MSV 5 Room_Hum_Source = 2 and MSV 6 Duct_Hum_Source = 3 (remote) the BMS writes AV 2 Room_Humidity and AV 4 Duct_Humidity; MSV 4 SP_Source = 3 takes the setpoint from BACnet.
