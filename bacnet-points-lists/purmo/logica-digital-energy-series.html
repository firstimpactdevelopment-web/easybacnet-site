Short answer

LOGICA Digital is a digital valve actuator for Purmo TOV valves that talks Modbus RTU or BACnet MS/TP. Over BACnet it takes a 0-100 % flow command or runs its own thermal-power or return-temperature control, reads two sensor inputs (P1/P2) for delta-T and energy, and reports position, estimated flow, power, energy and fault flags. Valve type, max flow and the control mode are set through writable objects.

This page lists 69 BACnet objects for the Purmo LOGICA Digital Energy-series actuator DN10-DN50 (BACnet MS/TP) — 43 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from a copy of Purmo’s documentation hosted by Internet Archive (copy of purmo.com), not by Purmo itself, so treat it as unofficial.

BMS integration notes: Purmo LOGICA Digital Energy-series actuator DN10-DN50 (BACnet MS/TP)

Integration in brief

Set the MS/TP address on the DIP switches (19 200 baud default), pick the valve with MV 8 and its max flow in AV 30, then choose the operating mode in MV 4. In the default mode the BMS writes AV 1 (0-100 % relative flow); for energy control it writes the power or return-temperature setpoint instead. Read actual flow, position, power and the fault BIs, and set MV 6 for what happens on bus loss.

Enable & remote start

MV 4 Driftsätt selects the control source: 1 = external signal on AV 1 (default), 8 = thermal power (AV 19), 9 = return temperature (AV 20); 2/3 force fully open or closed.

Heartbeat & communication loss

MV 6 Kommunikationsfel sets the action after 120 s without bus traffic: no change, close, open, or go to the emergency position in AV 8 (default 30 %).

Sensors & values from the BMS

Supply and return temperatures come from P1/P2 sensors or from the bus: MV 5 picks the source, and with bus values the BMS writes AV 4 (supply) and AV 5 (return).

Network setup

  • MS/TP address is set on the DIP switches; AV 28 can only be written when the DIP address is 63.
  • Default baud rate 19 200; change it with MV 7, then restart the bus with MV 1 = 6.
  • Select the valve type in MV 8 and set AV 30 to the max flow that matches the manual valve presetting.
  • Sensor types for P1/P2 are set in MV 2/MV 3 (e.g. 7 = Pt1000).

Watch out for

  • AV 4/AV 5 are read-only once a P1/P2 sensor is assigned as the temperature source.
  • Limit settings AV 22-24 use 0 to switch the limit off.
  • Writing 0 to AV 34 resets the continuous energy counter.
  • Object names in this list are the Swedish guide's labels.

Integration notes

  • Object names are the Swedish labels printed in the Swedish edition of the integration guide (APR 22); the names stored in the actuator may differ. Descriptions here are in English.
  • The guide marks AV 4, AV 5 and AV 28 as R/(W): AV 4/5 are writable only when no P1/P2 sensor source is assigned, AV 28 only when the DIP address is 63.
  • MSV objects are listed as MV. MV 8 has two state lists, one for DN10-32 and one for DN40-50 actuators.
  • Hardware version and RTC time are properties of the device object, and the serial number a property of AI 1; they are not separate objects.
  • AI 9 is printed across a page break: it counts hours until flushing starts (0-4320), then the minutes left of a running flush (0-600). Units for AI 2/AI 3 follow the selected sensor type.
  • Some state texts are machine-translated in the guide (e.g. 'Fr.o.m'/'T.o.m' for off/on, 'Sida 2' where P2 is meant); they are kept as printed.

How many BACnet points does the Purmo LOGICA Digital Energy-series actuator DN10-DN50 (BACnet MS/TP) have?

Which Purmo LOGICA Digital Energy-series actuator DN10-DN50 (BACnet MS/TP) BACnet points are writable?

43 of the 69 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.
