Senva TotalSense air quality sensor (BACnet MS/TP) BACnet points list

Updated 6 November 2026

Short answer

TotalSense is a Senva indoor air quality monitor with BACnet MS/TP. Analog Inputs give temperature, RH, dew point, CO2, TVOC, PM mass and counts, CO, ozone, light, occupancy, pressure, an air quality index and per-sensor status flags. Analog Values hold offsets, CO2 auto-calibration, relay and analog output setup, a built-in PID loop, air quality thresholds and display options.

BMS integration notes: Senva TotalSense air quality sensor (BACnet MS/TP)

Integration in brief

Set protocol, MAC and baud on the DIP switches, then read temperature, RH, CO2, PM2.5, TVOC and the air quality index. Check the per-sensor status AIs for faults.

Network setup

  • Protocol, MAC address (0-127), baud (9600-115200) and data/parity/stop come from DIP switches.
  • Device instance ships as 655xxx (last 3 digits of the serial number) and is writable; Max_Master is writable.

Watch out for

  • AV 31 = 1234 restores factory settings.
  • TVOC ppb assumes ethanol's molecular weight; use AV 8 to rescale for another compound.

Integration notes

  • Points from the Readings, Diagnostics and Settings tables of guide 154-0043-0G.
  • Objects for sensors a model does not have (CO, ozone, pressure, PIR, light, relay, analog output) are not active on that model.
  • The guide reuses names for AV 77-80 ("AQI Temp Range Low", "AQI CO Good quality"); their descriptions say they are the temperature and humidity good-quality window limits. Kept as printed.
  • Relay on/off thresholds AV 17/18 are percent of the selected sensor's full scale.
  • Protocol, MAC address, baud and parity (AV 32-35) are read from DIP switches and are read-only.

Questions about the Senva TotalSense air quality sensor (BACnet MS/TP)

How many BACnet points does the Senva TotalSense air quality sensor (BACnet MS/TP) have?

This list has 113 BACnet objects: 35 analog inputs, 78 analog values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Senva TotalSense air quality sensor (BACnet MS/TP) BACnet points are writable?

69 of the 113 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Senva TotalSense air quality sensor (BACnet MS/TP) BACnet documentation?

The source is BACnet Protocol Guide TotalSense Series (154-0043-0G), revision 2025-12-09, published free by Senva at https://www.senvainc.com/catalog/documents/downloadcenter/154-0043-0G%20TotalSense%20BACnet%20Guide.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Senva TotalSense air quality sensor (BACnet MS/TP)?

Start with AI 1 Temperature (Space temperature (units per AV 2)); AI 2 Relative humidity (Space RH); AI 3 CO2 (Space CO2, ppm); AI 7 PM 2.5 ug/m3 (PM2.5, µg/m3); AI 4 TVOC ug/m3 (TVOC, µg/m3); AI 10 Air quality (0 Good, 1 Fair, 2 Poor); AI 12 Occupancy (PIR occupancy (PIR models)); AI 17 System status (System fault bit flags).
