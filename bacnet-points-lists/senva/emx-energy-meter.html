Short answer

The EMX is a Senva three-phase energy meter. Analog Inputs give voltages, currents, power factor, frequency, real/reactive/apparent power and energy per phase and total, alarm and load status, pulse counts and the clock; Large Analog Values carry net, import and export energy totals. Multi-State and Analog Values set wiring, CT type, pulse outputs, alarms, scaling, clock and the on-board logger.

BMS integration notes: Senva EMX energy meter (BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP)

Integration in brief

Set wiring (MV 1), current element type (MV 2) and scaling (AV 1, AV 2) to match the installation, then read total power, energy, voltage, current and the alarm status. The meter is monitoring-only apart from resets and setup.

Network setup

  • EMX/EMX-L: BACnet MS/TP; EMX-IP: BACnet IP. Device instance defaults to 1 and is writable.
  • Wiring (MV 1), CT or Rogowski coil (MV 2), CT orientation (MV 3) and current/voltage scale (AV 1, AV 2) must match the install.
  • The EMX-L has no display; it is set up with the SenvaSync phone app.

Watch out for

  • AV 17 resets the energy totals; AV 20 clears the log.
  • Clock changes (AV 21-27) take effect only after writing 1 to AV 28.
  • Setup values are stored in non-volatile memory; do not write them cyclically.

Integration notes

  • Points from the Setup Registers and Logging Registers tables of BACnet guide 154-0041 rev0E.
  • EMX and EMX-L use BACnet MS/TP; EMX-IP uses BACnet IP. Pulse output MSVs 4-9 do not apply to the EMX-IP, and AV 20-AV 28 (log reset and clock set) do not exist on the EMX-L.
  • The guide names AI 33-35 "Net A/B/C Apparent Energy" in Wh, the same names as AI 39-41 in VAh; kept as printed.
  • Logging registers (AV 29-45, AI 49-78) read back entries from the on-board log.
  • Multistate objects are MSV in the guide; listed here as MV. All MSVs and AVs are read/write; AIs and LAVs are read-only.

Questions about the Senva EMX energy meter (BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP)

How many BACnet points does the Senva EMX energy meter (BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP) have?

This list has 199 BACnet objects: 103 analog inputs, 44 analog values, 16 multi-state values, 36 large analog values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Senva EMX energy meter (BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP) BACnet points are writable?

60 of the 199 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Senva EMX energy meter (BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP) BACnet documentation?

The source is EMX BACnet Guide (154-0041 rev0E), published free by Senva at https://www.senvainc.com/catalog/documents/downloadcenter/154-0041%20EMX%20BACnet%20Guide.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Senva EMX energy meter (BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP)?

Start with AI 5 Total Real Power (Total real power, W); LAV 1 Real Net Energy Total (Net real energy, Wh (EMX-IP only)); AI 8 Total Net Real Energy (Net real energy, Wh); AI 1 Average L-N Voltage (Average line-to-neutral voltage); AI 3 Average Current (Average phase current); AI 23 A Frequency (Frequency, Hz); AI 42 Alarm Status (Alarm bit field).

How do I check these points on the real EMX energy meter (BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP)?

Scan the network with a BACnet IP browser such as Easy BACnet, open the device and compare its object list with this table. Anything missing is usually an option that isn't installed or a point that is disabled in the controller's configuration.
