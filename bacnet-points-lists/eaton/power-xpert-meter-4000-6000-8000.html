Short answer

The PXM 4000/6000/8000 meters serve one BACnet IP device with volts, currents, power, power factor, energy, demand, THD and frequency as Analog Inputs, min / max and peak time stamps as DateTime Values, and the last events as CharacterString Values. The three models share the same object list; a few objects need the auxiliary voltage card or a PXM 6000/8000.

This page lists 426 BACnet objects for the Eaton Power Xpert Meter 4000 / 6000 / 8000 (PXM 4000/6000/8000) — 0 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from Eaton’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: Eaton Power Xpert Meter 4000 / 6000 / 8000 (PXM 4000/6000/8000)

Discover the meter over BACnet IP and read total real power, energy, demand, power factor, average volts and currents, and frequency. Everything is read-only monitoring data.

Network setup

  • BACnet IP (Annex J); the meter can register as a foreign device for BACnet networks across subnets.

Watch out for

  • Auxiliary-card voltage objects read only when that card is fitted.
  • Sag, swell and transient counters exist only on the PXM 6000 and 8000.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Eaton Power Xpert Meter 4000 / 6000 / 8000 (PXM 4000/6000/8000)?

Start with AI 1403 /mWtotal (Total real power); AI 1900 /mForwardEnergy (Forward real energy); AI 1948 /mDemandForwardWatts (Present forward watts demand); AI 1610 /mPFapparentTotal (System apparent power factor); AI 208 /mACVavgLN (Average line-to-neutral volts); AI 204 /mACVavgLL (Average line-to-line volts); AI 806 /mACIavg (Average phase current); AI 3400 /mFreq (Line frequency); MI 303 /sEntAlarmState (Meter alarm state).
