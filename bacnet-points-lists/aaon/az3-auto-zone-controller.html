Updated 12 November 2026

Short answer

The Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) controller runs an AAON packaged unit, either alone or as the air source for an AZ 3 zoning system. It has an on-board BACnet MS/TP port. The object map has 35 read-only analog inputs, 69 writable analog values (setpoints, offsets, overrides and BAS-supplied sensor values) and 59 read-only binary inputs (enable status, alarms and relay status).

This page lists 163 BACnet objects for the AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller — 69 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from AAON’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller

Integration in brief

Connect the on-board MS/TP port and set address, device ID and baud. The BAS schedules the unit with AV 62 Schedule Force, adjusts the occupied heating and cooling setpoints, and can override HVAC mode, fan VFD and OA damper or supply space and outdoor values through AV 66-69. Read the alarm status AI and the alarm BIs.

Enable & remote start

AV 62 Schedule Force sets occupancy (0 = controller schedule, 1 = forced occupied, 2 = forced unoccupied). AV 63 overrides the HVAC mode and AV 64 and AV 65 override fan VFD and OA damper; writing Auto (0, or 65535 for the VFD and damper) hands control back.

Sensors & values from the BMS

Sensor inputs cannot be written; the BAS writes AV 66 BACnet Space Temperature, AV 67 BACnet Space Humidity, AV 68 BACnet Outdoor Air Temperature and AV 69 BACnet Outdoor Air Humidity.

Network setup

  • MAC address (0-254, default 0), device ID (up to seven digits) and MS/TP baud rate (9600-76800, default 38400) are set on the controller's LCD Settings screens.

Watch out for

  • Setpoints written over BACnet become permanent only at midnight; a power cycle before then loses them.

Integration notes

  • AI and BI objects are read-only; AV objects are read/write. Sensor inputs cannot be written directly; the BAS writes AV 66-69 instead.
  • A setpoint written over BACnet is held in temporary memory until midnight, then saved as the new default; a power cycle before midnight loses it.
  • AI 2-4 and AI 31 are coded values; the bit meanings are in the guide's tables on pages 95-96.
  • Analog value units are not printed for most points; ranges are shown as printed.
  • Source: Appendix C AZ 3 BACnet Parameters, guide pages 88-94.

Questions about the AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller

How many BACnet points does the AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller have?

This list has 163 BACnet objects: 35 analog inputs, 69 analog values, 59 binary inputs. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller BACnet points are writable?

69 of the 163 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller BACnet documentation?

The source is Auto-Zone 3 Controller Technical Guide (G071000 Rev. 01B), revision 2021-01, published free by AAON at https://www.aaon.com/hubfs/Controls%20Literature/G071000-AZ3-TGD-01B%20210114.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller?

Start with AV 62 Schedule Force (Occupancy: 0 auto, 1 occupied, 2 unoccupied); AV 1 Occupied Cooling Setpoint (Cooling starts one degree above this); AV 2 Occupied Heating Setpoint (Heating starts one degree below this); AV 10 Supply Air Cooling Setpoint (Discharge air target in cooling); AI 3 Control Status (Current occupancy status); AI 4 HVAC Mode (Current operating mode); AI 7 Supply Air Temperature (Discharge air temperature); AI 31 Alarm Status (Alarm status (coded)).

How do I enable BMS control of the AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller?

AV 62 Schedule Force sets occupancy (0 = controller schedule, 1 = forced occupied, 2 = forced unoccupied). AV 63 overrides the HVAC mode and AV 64 and AV 65 override fan VFD and OA damper; writing Auto (0, or 65535 for the VFD and damper) hands control back.

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the AAON Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller?

Sensor inputs cannot be written; the BAS writes AV 66 BACnet Space Temperature, AV 67 BACnet Space Humidity, AV 68 BACnet Outdoor Air Temperature and AV 69 BACnet Outdoor Air Humidity.

How do I check these points on the real Auto-Zone 3 (AZ 3) Controller?

Scan the network with a BACnet IP browser such as Easy BACnet, open the device and compare its object list with this table. Anything missing is usually an option that isn't installed or a point that is disabled in the controller's configuration.
