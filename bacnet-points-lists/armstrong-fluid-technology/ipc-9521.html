BMS integration notes: Armstrong Fluid Technology IPC 9521 chilled water plant controller

Integration in brief

The IPC 9521 publishes a fixed BACnet object list. Start and stop the plant with BO 1 Remote Start, write the zone setpoint (AV 2), and read BI 1 Plant Status, plant load, chilled water temperatures, plant power and efficiency. Use BO 3 to reset alarms. Per-chiller, per-pump and per-valve alarms, run feedbacks and Hand/Auto overrides are there if the BMS needs more detail.

Enable & remote start

BO 1 Remote Start (0 = Stop, 1 = Start) starts and stops the plant from the BMS. The data-points sheet says nothing more about local/remote selection.

Network setup

  • The data-points sheet does not give the device instance, MAC address, baud rate or IP settings; get them from the controller setup.

Watch out for

  • BO 3 Alarm Reset acts on a positive edge (0 to 1); write 0 again before the next reset.
  • Hand/Auto BVs and Hand Command / Hand Speed objects override the plant sequence; leave them in Auto for normal operation.
  • Mode AVs are coded 0-3 (1 = Hand, 2 = Off, 3 = Auto); writing them takes that equipment out of automatic sequencing.
  • Set Lead Chiller / Pump objects act only when the value changes, so rewriting the same value does nothing.

Integration notes

  • The data-points sheet titles the table BACnet but does not say whether it is BACnet IP or MS/TP.
  • Mode objects (AV) use 0 = Not Used, 1 = Hand, 2 = Off, 3 = Auto. Duty/standby, chiller duty and chiller state objects are coded values; the codes are in each point's states.
  • Hand/Auto BVs and Hand Command BVs, plus the Hand Speed / Hand Position AOs, let the BMS run a valve, pump or fan by hand. Set Lead objects act only when the written value changes.
  • Units for temperatures, pressures and flows follow the controller's unit setting (the PDF lists the options).
  • Footnote asterisks after some pump names in the PDF are left off the object names. They mean: 1/1a is a single pump or side A of a dualArm/Twin pump, and the b points are side B (not used for single pumps).
  • The PDF table is on pages 15-25. One Reserved AI is left out.

Questions about the Armstrong Fluid Technology IPC 9521 chilled water plant controller

How many BACnet points does the Armstrong Fluid Technology IPC 9521 chilled water plant controller have?

This list has 468 BACnet objects: 231 analog inputs, 27 analog outputs, 36 analog values, 138 binary inputs, 4 binary outputs, 32 binary values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Armstrong Fluid Technology IPC 9521 chilled water plant controller BACnet points are writable?

99 of the 468 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Armstrong Fluid Technology IPC 9521 chilled water plant controller BACnet documentation?

The source is IPC 9521 Data points (Communication Interface Rev 18.122) (90.874), revision 2019-09-16, published free by Armstrong Fluid Technology at https://armstrongfluidtechnology.com/~/media/documents/installation_maintenance_parts/installation-and-operating-instructions/90-874_ipc9521_datapoints_iando.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Armstrong Fluid Technology IPC 9521 chilled water plant controller?

Start with BO 1 Remote Start (Plant start/stop from the BMS (1 = start)); AV 2 Zone 1 Setpoint (Zone 1 control setpoint); BO 3 Alarm Reset (Reset plant alarms (positive edge)); BI 1 Plant Status (Plant on/off status); AI 3 Plant Load (Plant cooling load, tons); AI 1001 chws Temp (Chilled water supply temperature); AI 1002 chwr Temp (Chilled water return temperature); AI 2 Total Plant Power Consumption (Total plant power, kW); AI 1 Plant Efficiency (Plant efficiency, kW/ton); BI 10 Refrigerant Leak Alarm (Refrigerant leak detected).

How do I enable BMS control of the Armstrong Fluid Technology IPC 9521 chilled water plant controller?

BO 1 Remote Start (0 = Stop, 1 = Start) starts and stops the plant from the BMS. The data-points sheet says nothing more about local/remote selection.
