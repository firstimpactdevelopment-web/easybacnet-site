BMS integration notes: Armstrong Fluid Technology Design Envelope Integrated Tower Controller (ITC 9521)

Integration in brief

The ITC 9521 runs the condenser water side: cooling towers, condenser pumps, tower isolation valves and bypass valves. Start and stop it with BO 1 Remote Start, read BI 1 Plant Status, tower water temperatures, condenser flow and outdoor conditions, and reset alarms with BO 2. Per-pump and per-fan modes, hand speeds and valve overrides are available.

Enable & remote start

BO 1 Remote Start (0 = Stop, 1 = Start) starts and stops the controller from the BMS. The data-points sheet says nothing more about local/remote selection.

Network setup

  • The data-points sheet does not give the device instance, MAC address, baud rate or IP settings; get them from the controller setup.

Watch out for

  • BO 2 Alarm Reset acts on a positive edge (0 to 1).
  • Mode/Selector AVs accept only 1-3 when written (1 = Hand, 2 = Off, 3 = Auto); Hand runs the pump or fan at its Hand Speed AO.
  • Hand/Auto switch BVs and Hand Command BVs override the valve sequence; leave them in Auto for normal operation.
  • The Sensorless Head points are printed with kW as the unit; treat that unit with care.

Questions about the Armstrong Fluid Technology Design Envelope Integrated Tower Controller (ITC 9521)

How many BACnet points does the Armstrong Fluid Technology Design Envelope Integrated Tower Controller (ITC 9521) have?

This list has 210 BACnet objects: 98 analog inputs, 10 analog outputs, 10 analog values, 68 binary inputs, 2 binary outputs, 22 binary values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Armstrong Fluid Technology Design Envelope Integrated Tower Controller (ITC 9521) BACnet points are writable?

44 of the 210 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Armstrong Fluid Technology Design Envelope Integrated Tower Controller (ITC 9521) BACnet documentation?

The source is Design Envelope Integrated Tower Controller Data points (BACnet Communication Interface Rev 17.111) (82.21), revision 2018-07-17, published free by Armstrong Fluid Technology at https://armstrongfluidtechnology.com/~/media/documents/installation_maintenance_parts/installation-and-operating-instructions/82-21_designenvelope_itc9521_datapoints.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Armstrong Fluid Technology Design Envelope Integrated Tower Controller (ITC 9521)?

Start with BO 1 Remote Start (Start/stop from the BMS (1 = start)); BO 2 Alarm Reset (Reset alarms (positive edge)); BI 1 Plant Status (Controller on/off status); AI 1 Leaving Tower Water Temp (Condenser water leaving the towers); AI 2 Entering Tower Water Temp (Condenser water entering the towers); AI 3 Condenser Flow (Condenser water flow); AI 4 Outside Air Temp (Dry Bulb) (Outdoor dry-bulb temperature); BI 128 Cooling Tower Water Low Level Alarm (Tower basin low water level).

How do I enable BMS control of the Armstrong Fluid Technology Design Envelope Integrated Tower Controller (ITC 9521)?

BO 1 Remote Start (0 = Stop, 1 = Start) starts and stops the controller from the BMS. The data-points sheet says nothing more about local/remote selection.
