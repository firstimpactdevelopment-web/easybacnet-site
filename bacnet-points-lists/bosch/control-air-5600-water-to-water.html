BMS integration notes: Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Water (WWZ software)

Integration in brief

Leave Control Source at BAS Occupancy Command, command occupancy with occupancy_cmd_1, pick the control mode (heat, cool, auto changeover or digital input) and write the occupied setpoints. Watch current_alarm_1 and alrm_status_1 plus the load and leaving water temperatures.

Enable & remote start

Set ctrl_source_1 (AV 16) to 2 = BAS Occupancy Command (default) and write occupancy_cmd_1 (BV 1). ctrl_mode_1 (AV 17) defaults to 4 = digital input (off = heat, on = cool), so set it to heat, cool or auto changeover for network control.

Network setup

  • BACnet MS/TP runs on the EIA-485 port; the BAS Port Settings DIP switches select the protocol (BACnet MS/TP, N2, Modbus) and baud rate (9.6 to 76.8 kbps), the same for every controller on the trunk.
  • The two rotary dials set the MAC address (tens and ones); the controller only reads them at power-up, so change them with power off.
  • A Communication jumper selects EIA-485 (MS/TP, N2, Modbus, Lon) or BACnet over ARC156 at 156 kbps.
  • Setpoints and configuration can also be changed locally with a BACview terminal or Virtual BACview.

Watch out for

  • Default control mode follows a digital input; a BMS mode write does nothing useful until ctrl_mode_1 is changed.
  • Use either the °F (AV 1-7) or °C (AV 501-507) setpoint set consistently with temp_unit_mode_1.
  • Factory test BVs (91000-91005) force outputs; leave them alone.

Integration notes

  • Object names end in _1 as printed in the points list.
  • Setpoints exist twice: AV 1-7 in °F and AV 501-507 in °C; temp_unit_mode_1 (AV 550) selects the unit system.
  • BV 91000-91005 are factory end-of-line test points; do not write them in service.
  • The PDF repeats row numbers 60-65 for the factory test points; all rows are kept.

Questions about the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Water (WWZ software)

How many BACnet points does the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Water (WWZ software) have?

This list has 67 BACnet objects: 27 analog values, 40 binary values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Water (WWZ software) BACnet points are writable?

30 of the 67 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Water (WWZ software) BACnet documentation?

The source is Bosch TT - Water to Water Network Integration Points List (WWZ.1.03), published free by Bosch at https://www.bosch-homecomfort.com/us/media/country_pool/service/technical_guides/ipl_wwz_1_03.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Water (WWZ software)?

Start with BV 1 occupancy_cmd_1 (BAS occupied command); AV 16 ctrl_source_1 (Occupancy source; 2 = BAS occupancy command (default)); AV 17 ctrl_mode_1 (Off / heat / cool / auto changeover / digital input); AV 4 occ_clg_stpt_1 (Occupied cooling setpoint (°F)); AV 2 occ_htg_stpt_1 (Occupied heating setpoint (°F)); AV 10 eff_load_tmp_f_1 (Load water temperature); AV 9 eff_lvg_wtr_tmp_1 (Leaving water temperature); AV 19 current_alarm_1 (Active alarm code); BV 20 alrm_status_1 (Alarm present flag).

How do I enable BMS control of the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Water (WWZ software)?

Set ctrl_source_1 (AV 16) to 2 = BAS Occupancy Command (default) and write occupancy_cmd_1 (BV 1). ctrl_mode_1 (AV 17) defaults to 4 = digital input (off = heat, on = cool), so set it to heat, cool or auto changeover for network control.
