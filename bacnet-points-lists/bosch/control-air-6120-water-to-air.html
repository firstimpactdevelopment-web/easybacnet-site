Short answer

The Control Air 6120 is Bosch's DDC controller for larger commercial water-to-air heat pumps, including discharge-air control, VFD fans, economizer/boilerless options, hot gas reheat and auxiliary heat. It is a BACnet Advanced Application Controller on MS/TP, PTP or ARC156 (also Modbus and N2). Many points only apply with the Control Air 6120 Point Expander and the matching options.

BMS integration notes: Bosch Control Air 6120 - Water to Air (WAF software)

Integration in brief

Command occupancy over the network with occupancy_cmd_1 (Control Source = BAS), write occupied setpoints, and choose the temperature source; supply BAS values (zone temp, DAT, RH, CO2, OAT) if the BMS owns the sensors. Monitor current_alarm_1, alm_status_1 and sys_status_1.

Enable & remote start

Control Source (ctrl_source_1, AV 15) defaults to 2 = BAS Occupancy Command; write occupancy_cmd_1 (BV 1) to occupy the unit. Alternatives are digital input IN-1, keypad schedule, manual on or the ZS push-button override. loop_enabled_1 (BV 23) must be 1 to allow heating and cooling.

Sensors & values from the BMS

BMS values can be written to bas_sen_val_1 (AV 7, zone temperature), bas_dat_val_1 (AV 90, discharge air; select with dat_sel_1 AV 81 = 3), bas_rh_sen_val_1 (AV 56), bas_co2_val_1 (AV 49) and bas_oat_1 (AV 29).

Network setup

  • BACnet MS/TP runs on the EIA-485 port; the BAS Port Settings DIP switches select the protocol (BACnet MS/TP, N2, Modbus) and baud rate (9.6 to 76.8 kbps), the same for every controller on the trunk.
  • The two rotary dials set the MAC address (tens and ones); the controller only reads them at power-up, so change them with power off.
  • Port 2a is set to EIA-485 or EIA-232 and 2-wire or 4-wire with jumpers; Port 1 is ARC156 only.
  • The controller is a BACnet Advanced Application Controller (B-AAC).

Watch out for

  • High static shutdowns follow a three-strike rule; once locked out (code 100) the unit can only be reset from the BACview terminal, not over BACnet.
  • Wall-sensor setpoint adjustment is limited to ±3 °F by stpt_adj_lim_1.
  • Auxiliary heat (and several other options) needs the Control Air 6120 Point Expander.
  • Configuration AVs (input selectors, unit mode, stages, coil configuration) are commissioning settings; avoid cyclic writes.

Integration notes

  • Object names end in _1 as printed; the LP3 alarm is printed with the name x_1 (BV 6).
  • Alarms are reported through current_alarm_1 (AV 17); high-static lockout is reported as code 100 on sys_status_1 (AV 16) and can only be reset from BACview.
  • Points for compressor stages 3-4, auxiliary heat 2-4 and second UPM only apply to units built with them.
  • AV 2001-2005 hold factory serial / part number data.

Questions about the Bosch Control Air 6120 - Water to Air (WAF software)

How many BACnet points does the Bosch Control Air 6120 - Water to Air (WAF software) have?

This list has 200 BACnet objects: 1 analog input, 96 analog values, 103 binary values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Bosch Control Air 6120 - Water to Air (WAF software) BACnet points are writable?

70 of the 200 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Bosch Control Air 6120 - Water to Air (WAF software) BACnet documentation?

The source is Bosch TT - Water to Air Network Integration Points List (WAF.1.01), revision 2019-08-12, published free by Bosch at https://www.bosch-homecomfort.com/us/media/country_pool/service/technical_guides/ipl_waf_1_01_us.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Bosch Control Air 6120 - Water to Air (WAF software)?

Start with BV 1 occupancy_cmd_1 (BAS occupied command; 1 enables unit operation); AV 15 ctrl_source_1 (Occupancy source; 2 = BAS occupancy command (default)); AV 4 occ_clg_stpt_1 (Occupied cooling setpoint); AV 2 occ_htg_stpt_1 (Occupied heating setpoint); AV 16 sys_status_1 (Unoccupied / occupied / fan only / heating / cooling state); AV 57 eff_zone_temp_1 (Zone temperature in use); AV 17 current_alarm_1 (Active alarm code); BV 24 alm_status_1 (Alarm present flag).

How do I enable BMS control of the Bosch Control Air 6120 - Water to Air (WAF software)?

Control Source (ctrl_source_1, AV 15) defaults to 2 = BAS Occupancy Command; write occupancy_cmd_1 (BV 1) to occupy the unit. Alternatives are digital input IN-1, keypad schedule, manual on or the ZS push-button override. loop_enabled_1 (BV 23) must be 1 to allow heating and cooling.

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the Bosch Control Air 6120 - Water to Air (WAF software)?

BMS values can be written to bas_sen_val_1 (AV 7, zone temperature), bas_dat_val_1 (AV 90, discharge air; select with dat_sel_1 AV 81 = 3), bas_rh_sen_val_1 (AV 56), bas_co2_val_1 (AV 49) and bas_oat_1 (AV 29).
