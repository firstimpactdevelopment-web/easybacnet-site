Short answer

The Control Air 5600 is Bosch's factory-mounted DDC controller for water-source heat pumps. It speaks BACnet MS/TP (or BACnet over ARC156, Modbus, N2 and Lon) and exposes occupancy, mode, setpoint, sensor, configuration and alarm points as AV/BV/MSV objects. The same point list also documents Modbus registers and N2/LON equivalents.

BMS integration notes: Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Air (WAZ software)

Integration in brief

Leave Control Source at its default (BAS Occupancy Command), then command occupied/unoccupied with occupancy_cmd_1 and set the occupied cooling/heating setpoints. Choose the zone temperature source (ZS sensor, remote sensor or BAS value) and, if the BMS supplies the temperature, write bas_sen_val_1. Monitor current_alarm_1, alm_status_1 and the effective zone temperature.

Enable & remote start

Set ctrl_source_1 (AV 15) to 2 = BAS Occupancy Command (factory default) and write occupancy_cmd_1 (BV 1) = 1 to occupy the unit. Other sources are a digital input enable, keypad schedule or manual on. The BAS loop enable point (loop_enabled_1, BV 23) must be 1 to allow heating/cooling; it defaults to 1.

Heartbeat & communication loss

No communication timeout is described. If the BAS temperature source is used and nothing is ever written, the zone temperature defaults to 74 °F; after that the last written value is used indefinitely.

Sensors & values from the BMS

Set the compressor control mode (comp_mode_1, AV 64) to 2 = Zone Control - BAS Sensor and write the zone temperature to bas_sen_val_1 (AV 19). BAS humidity goes to bas_rh_sen_val_1 (AV 56) with rh_bas_sel_1 (AV 51); BAS CO2 to bas_co2_val_1 (AV 49) with co2_bas_ena_1 (AV 106); mixed air to bas_mat_1 (AV 86); outdoor air to effective_oat_1 (AV 29).

Network setup

  • BACnet MS/TP runs on the EIA-485 port; the BAS Port Settings DIP switches select the protocol (BACnet MS/TP, N2, Modbus) and baud rate (9.6 to 76.8 kbps), the same for every controller on the trunk.
  • The two rotary dials set the MAC address (tens and ones); the controller only reads them at power-up, so change them with power off.
  • A Communication jumper selects EIA-485 (MS/TP, N2, Modbus, Lon) or BACnet over ARC156 at 156 kbps.
  • Setpoints and configuration can also be changed locally with a BACview terminal or Virtual BACview.

Watch out for

  • With a ZS or remote sensor selected, the controller looks for it during the first 30 s after power-up; if missing, the unit stays unoccupied, alarms and shows -60 °F.
  • Writing bas_smoke_1 = 1 shuts the supply fan, compressors and reheat valve off.
  • The input/output configuration AVs (in1/in2/ao/bo cfg) change how hardware I/O is used; treat them as commissioning settings, not operating points.
  • There is a 1-minute delay when the unit changes between heating and cooling.

Questions about the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Air (WAZ software)

How many BACnet points does the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Air (WAZ software) have?

This list has 174 BACnet objects: 88 analog values, 82 binary values, 4 multi-state values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Air (WAZ software) BACnet points are writable?

73 of the 174 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Air (WAZ software) BACnet documentation?

The source is Bosch TT - Water to Air Network Integration Points List (WAZ.1.03), published free by Bosch at https://www.bosch-homecomfort.com/us/media/country_pool/service/technical_guides/ipl_waz_1_03.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Air (WAZ software)?

Start with BV 1 occupancy_cmd_1 (BAS occupied command; 1 enables unit operation); AV 15 ctrl_source_1 (Occupancy source; 2 = BAS occupancy command (default)); MV 3 zone_mode_1 (Auto / heat / cool / off); AV 4 occ_clg_stpt_1 (Occupied cooling setpoint); AV 2 occ_htg_stpt_1 (Occupied heating setpoint); AV 16 sys_status_1 (Unoccupied / occupied / fan only / heating / cooling state); AV 7 eff_zone_temp_1 (Zone temperature in use); AV 17 current_alarm_1 (Active alarm code); BV 24 alm_status_1 (Alarm present flag).

How do I enable BMS control of the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Air (WAZ software)?

Set ctrl_source_1 (AV 15) to 2 = BAS Occupancy Command (factory default) and write occupancy_cmd_1 (BV 1) = 1 to occupy the unit. Other sources are a digital input enable, keypad schedule or manual on. The BAS loop enable point (loop_enabled_1, BV 23) must be 1 to allow heating/cooling; it defaults to 1.

Does the Bosch Control Air 5600 - Water to Air (WAZ software) need a BACnet heartbeat or have a communication timeout?

No communication timeout is described. If the BAS temperature source is used and nothing is ever written, the zone temperature defaults to 74 °F; after that the last written value is used indefinitely.
