BMS integration notes: Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option)

Integration in brief

The BMS talks BACnet MS/TP to the UPC Open, which reads and writes the ComfortLink control over CCN. Clear Local Machine Disable, set the CCN schedule number for the occupancy source you want, then write occupancy, setpoints and (for VAV) the supply air setpoint; read HVAC mode, temperatures and alarm state. Values the BMS feeds in (space, return or outdoor temperature, RH, CO2) are forces that clear if not refreshed.

Enable & remote start

The unit ships with Local Machine Disable (Service Test > STOP, BV 59 unitstop_1) set to Yes; set it to No before the unit runs. Occupancy follows the ComfortLink CCN schedule number (Configuration > CCN > SC.OV > SCH.N): 0 = always occupied, 1 = local schedule (default), 65-99 = a CCN schedule. The points list makes Occupied? (BV 2008) writable, but the manual does not describe how a write interacts with SCH.N; test it on site.

Heartbeat & communication loss

The manual says forces at CCN priority 7 clear unless rewritten in time: outside air temperature, outside RH, outside air quality and static pressure reset after 30 minutes, return air and space temperature after 3 minutes.

Sensors & values from the BMS

Space Temperature (AV 137), Return Air Temperature (AV 135), Outside Air Temperature (AV 1003), Outside Air RH (AV 119), IAQ (AV 1009) and OAQ (AV 113) are writable. Refresh them within the force timeouts (3 min for space and return temperature, 30 min for outdoor values).

Network setup

  • Set the MAC address on the UPC Open's Tens and Ones rotary switches; the controller reads switches, DIP switches and jumpers only at power-up.
  • Device instance = network number (default 16101) followed by the MAC address, e.g. MAC 20 gives 1610120.
  • BAS port: DS3 enable, DS4 E1-485, DS5-DS8 set to MSTP; baud on DS1-DS2 (9600, 19.2k, 38.4k or 76.8k, Carrier default 76.8k); 8N1 fixed. EIA-485 jumpers to EIA-485 and 2W.
  • Fit a BT485 terminator on the first and last controller of the MS/TP segment.
  • The UPC Open and the ComfortLink board must use the same CCN element and bus numbers (factory 1 and 0).

Watch out for

  • Points depend on unit options (heat type, economizer, Humidi-MiZer, VFDs, building pressure control); objects for options not fitted carry no useful data.
  • The user-defined analog and binary values are spare objects with no unit function.

Questions about the Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option)

How many BACnet points does the Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option) have?

This list has 285 BACnet objects: 188 analog values, 97 binary values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option) BACnet points are writable?

121 of the 285 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option) BACnet documentation?

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option)?

(0 = no, 1 = yes); writable); AV 3001 occ_cl_stpt_1 (Occupied cooling setpoint); AV 3002 occ_ht_stpt_1 (Occupied heating setpoint); AV 3007 sa_temp_stpt_1 (Supply air setpoint (VAV units)); AV 1022 hvac_mode_1 (HVAC mode as a number); BV 2001 sfan_1 (Supply fan relay on/off); AV 43 ctrltemp_1 (Control temperature (RAT, SPT or zone)); AV 10 sat_1 (Supply air temperature leaving the fan); BV 9 alm_1 (Unit alarm state); BV 105 comm_lost_alm_1 (UPC lost CCN communication with the unit).

How do I enable BMS control of the Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option)?

The unit ships with Local Machine Disable (Service Test > STOP, BV 59 unitstop_1) set to Yes; set it to No before the unit runs. Occupancy follows the ComfortLink CCN schedule number (Configuration > CCN > SC.OV > SCH.N): 0 = always occupied, 1 = local schedule (default), 65-99 = a CCN schedule. The points list makes Occupied? (BV 2008) writable, but the manual does not describe how a write interacts with SCH.N; test it on site.

Does the Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option) need a BACnet heartbeat or have a communication timeout?

The manual says forces at CCN priority 7 clear unless rewritten in time: outside air temperature, outside RH, outside air quality and static pressure reset after 30 minutes, return air and space temperature after 3 minutes.

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the Carrier Weathermaster 48/50P2-P5 030-100 Large Rooftop (ComfortLink 7.x, UPC Open BACnet option)?

Space Temperature (AV 137), Return Air Temperature (AV 135), Outside Air Temperature (AV 1003), Outside Air RH (AV 119), IAQ (AV 1009) and OAQ (AV 113) are writable. Refresh them within the force timeouts (3 min for space and return temperature, 30 min for outdoor values).
