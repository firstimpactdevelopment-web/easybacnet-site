Strato Automation OPENSTAT OS537H-EH electric heat staging thermostat BACnet points list

Updated 12 November 2026

Short answer

The OS537H-EH controls electric heat with up to six on/off stages and a modulating vernier stage (SSR pulsed or SCR 0-10 V), from room temperature, supply temperature, both, or an external 2-10 V BMS signal. Its BACnet MS/TP objects give setpoints, staging settings, stage demands and statuses, the effective stage count and two PID loops.

BMS integration notes: Strato Automation OPENSTAT OS537H-EH electric heat staging thermostat

Integration in brief

Write the room setpoint (AV-1), and the supply setpoint (AV-2) for supply or dual control. Read room and supply temperatures, heating demand and the stage statuses. With MSV-3 = ExtBMS a 2-10 V signal on AI-1 sets the heating demand directly.

Minimum points to map

The short list most integrators start with — enough to run the unit from a BMS. Map more as the job needs.

Enable & remote start

MSV-3 CtrlTyp = ExtBMS makes a 2-10 VDC signal on AI-1 drive the heating demand (AV-101, 0-100 %) directly.

Network setup

  • MS/TP address and baud are set in the MSTP Config menu.
  • MSV-1 sets the number of stages and MSV-2 the vernier ratio.

Watch out for

  • AV-3 sets the minimum interstage and on/off delays; BV-1 to 6 show stage demand before delays, BV-21 to 26 after.

Integration notes

  • The guide has no read/write column. Access here follows its tags: Cfg, User and Cmd objects are shown writable, Status objects read-only, and BO/AO objects commandable (the guide lists them for output overrides).
  • AO-7 is printed with the object name BO_7_SSR (the 24 Vac pulsed SSR output).
  • AV-100 is printed with the range 50-120°F although it is a percentage demand.
  • Multistate state numbers follow the order the states are printed, starting at 1.

Questions about the Strato Automation OPENSTAT OS537H-EH electric heat staging thermostat

How many BACnet points does the Strato Automation OPENSTAT OS537H-EH electric heat staging thermostat have?

This list has 61 BACnet objects: 7 analog inputs, 4 analog outputs, 16 analog values, 6 binary outputs, 12 binary values, 2 loops, 14 multi-state values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Strato Automation OPENSTAT OS537H-EH electric heat staging thermostat BACnet points are writable?

30 of the 61 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done. The source has no read/write column, so this is inferred from object type.

Where is the official Strato Automation OPENSTAT OS537H-EH electric heat staging thermostat BACnet documentation?

The source is OS537H-EH Technical User Guide, BACnet Objects, revision Sept 2024 rev 0, published free by Strato Automation at https://stratoautomation.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/OS537H-EH-Technical-User-Guide-Sept-2024-rev-0.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Strato Automation OPENSTAT OS537H-EH electric heat staging thermostat?

Start with AV 1 RoomSetp (Room temperature setpoint); AV 2 Supp_SP (Supply temperature setpoint); MV 3 CtrlTyp (Control type: Intern, Supply, Dual, ExtBMS); AI 6 RoomT (Room temperature); AI 3 AI_3_Supply (Supply temperature); AV 101 Htg_Dem (Heating demand, %); MV 101 EffStgQty (Stages on).

How do I enable BMS control of the Strato Automation OPENSTAT OS537H-EH electric heat staging thermostat?
