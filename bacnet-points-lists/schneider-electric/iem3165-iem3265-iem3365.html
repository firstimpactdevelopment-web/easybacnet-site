Updated 28 October 2026

Short answer

The BACnet versions of the iEM3000 DIN-rail meters (iEM3165 63 A direct, iEM3265 CT-connected, iEM3365 125 A direct) are BACnet MS/TP B-ASC devices. They serve Analog Inputs for current, voltage, power, power factor, frequency, total, partial, per-phase and per-tariff energy, plus meter and I/O configuration readbacks; one Analog Value for commands; and seven Binary Inputs for I/O and the overload alarm. The iEM3165 and iEM3365 stop at AI 48; AI 49-55 (VT/CT settings) are iEM3265 only.

BMS integration notes: Schneider Electric Acti 9 iEM3165 / iEM3265 / iEM3365 energy meter (BACnet MS/TP)

Integration in brief

At the front panel set the MS/TP baud rate, MAC address and device ID, then read the energy import total (AI 27), total active power (AI 22), current, voltage and power factor. Read BI 05 if the overload alarm is used. Write AV00 only to reset partial counters or acknowledge alarms.

Network setup

  • Set baud (9600, 19200, 38400, 57600 or 76800), MAC address (1-127) and device ID (0-4194303) at the front panel.
  • The device ID can only be set at the front panel; Object_Name, Description and Location are writable over BACnet.
  • Max_Master and Max_Info_Frames are writable; APDU timeout 1000-30000 ms.
  • No segmentation; max APDU 480 bytes.

Watch out for

  • Energy is reported in Wh, not kWh.
  • AI 49-55 exist only on the CT-connected iEM3265.
  • The comms LED flashes even when there is a communication error.

Integration notes

  • Configuration readbacks (AI 0-6, AI 44-55) can't be changed over BACnet; set them at the front panel.
  • Energy values are in Wh and survive power loss.
  • AV00 Command accepts 20001 (acknowledge overload alarm), 2020 (reset partial energy) and 2023 (reset input metering).
  • Up to 14 COV subscriptions on AI and BI objects.
  • Object names follow the manual's 'AIxx - name' form.
  • Source is user manual DOCA0005EN-16, chapter 'Communications using BACnet'.

Questions about the Schneider Electric Acti 9 iEM3165 / iEM3265 / iEM3365 energy meter (BACnet MS/TP)

How many BACnet points does the Schneider Electric Acti 9 iEM3165 / iEM3265 / iEM3365 energy meter (BACnet MS/TP) have?

This list has 64 BACnet objects: 56 analog inputs, 1 analog value, 7 binary inputs. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Schneider Electric Acti 9 iEM3165 / iEM3265 / iEM3365 energy meter (BACnet MS/TP) BACnet points are writable?

1 of the 64 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Schneider Electric Acti 9 iEM3165 / iEM3265 / iEM3365 energy meter (BACnet MS/TP)?
