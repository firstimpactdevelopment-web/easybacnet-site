Accuenergy AcuRev 4100 multi-circuit power meter (BACnet) BACnet points list

Updated 7 November 2026

Short answer

The AcuRev 4100 meters up to 24 CT input channels plus 12 user-defined summing channels. Real-time, demand and power quality values are read-only Analog Inputs (1-721) and energy totals are Large Analog Values (1-360). Channel 1 of each channel group is printed in full; the other channels follow the same layout in the ranges given in the notes.

BMS integration notes: Accuenergy AcuRev 4100 multi-circuit power meter (BACnet)

Integration in brief

Everything is read-only. Map the system power, energy and demand objects first, then the channel 1 objects and the matching objects for the other channels using the per-channel offsets in the notes.

Integration notes

  • Per-channel blocks: the list prints channel 1 and then a range for the rest - input channel basics AI 44-204 (7 per channel), user channel basics AI 211-276 (6 per channel; printed as 205-276), input channel demand AI 307-444, user channel demand AI 451-516, input channel power quality AI 546-637 (4 per channel), user channel power quality AI 645-721 (7 per channel), input channel energy LAV 46-252 and user channel energy LAV 262-360 (9 per channel). Only printed objects are listed here.
  • AI 208 (Susr1_rms) is printed with 'S' in the ID column; it sits between AI 207 and AI 209 and is listed as 208.
  • Load nature values: 0 resistive, 1 capacitive, 2 inductive.
  • The list does not say which BACnet transport the meter uses; check the meter manual.
  • Source: Accuenergy 'BACnet Points List AcuRev4100' spreadsheet, firmware v1.03p06, generated 2026-05-26.
