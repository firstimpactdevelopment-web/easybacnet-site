BMS integration notes: OJ Electronics OJ Air Plus AHU controller (EXcon-Plus), SW 1.09

Integration in brief

Read the unit state from AI 0 and BI 0, the temperatures and flows from the AI block, and the two alarm relays (BI 30 A-alarm, BI 31 B-alarm). BMS run modes go through AV 108, which only works after the physical operation input is activated; BV 0 resets alarms.

Enable & remote start

AV 108 sets the BMS run mode, but only after the physical operation input is activated (per the object name).

Sensors & values from the BMS

Outdoor and room temperature can come from the BMS: write AV 102 / AV 103 and switch them in with BV 15 / BV 16.

Network setup

  • The EDE gives device instance 15530 and device name EXcon-Plus.

Watch out for

  • Most temperatures and percentages are in hundredths.
  • Object names are long texts cut at about 63 characters; map by type and instance, not by name.
  • Outdoor and room temperature from the BMS (AV 102, AV 103) are only used when BV 15 / BV 16 activate them.
