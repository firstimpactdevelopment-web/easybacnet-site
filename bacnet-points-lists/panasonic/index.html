Panasonic BACnet points lists

Updated 16 October 2026

We haven’t found a public BACnet points list published by Panasonic itself. Panasonic units are usually put on BACnet through a protocol gateway, and the gateway makers do publish their object tables. These are the gateways we have for Panasonic, each with its full object list, the minimum points to map, and CSV/EDE downloads.

Short answer

To read or control Panasonic equipment from a BACnet BMS, fit a BACnet gateway on the unit’s own bus. Each indoor unit then appears as a set of BACnet objects (on/off, mode, setpoint, room temperature, fan speed, alarm) whose instance numbers follow the gateway’s numbering rule — see the integration notes on each list.

  • Multi-brand VRF / split AC BACnet gateway

    CoolAutomation CoolMasterNet BACnet (indoor unit objects)

    Indoor units of VRF and split systems connected to a CoolMasterNet or CooLink gateway (Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, LG, Samsung, Gree and other supported brands).

    16 objects · 12 writable
    Also known as: CoolMasterNet, CooLinkNet, CooLinkHub, CooLinkBridge, CoolAutomation BACnet, LG VRF BACnet gateway, Daikin VRV BACnet gateway, Mitsubishi Electric BACnet gateway, Samsung BACnet gateway, Gree GMV BACnet gateway
  • Air conditioner BACnet gateway (1 indoor unit)

    Intesis INBACPAN001R100 (Panasonic ECOi/PACi gateway)

    Panasonic ECOi (VRF) and PACi indoor units, via the R1/R2 remote-controller bus.

    46 objects · 18 writable · PA-RC2-BAC-1L (legacy code)
    Also known as: Panasonic BACnet gateway, Panasonic VRF BACnet, Panasonic ECOi BACnet, Panasonic PACi BACnet, Panasonic BACnet points list, PA-RC2-BAC-1L
  • VRF BACnet gateway (up to 16, 64 or 128 indoor units)

    Intesis INBACPAN016O000 / 064O000 / 128O000 (Panasonic VRF P-Link BACnet server)

    Panasonic VRF (ECOi and similar) systems on one or two P-Link lines, up to 128 indoor units depending on model.

    55 objects · 20 writable
    Also known as: Panasonic VRF BACnet gateway, Panasonic P-Link BACnet, Panasonic BACnet points list, PA-AC-BAC-16, PA-AC-BAC-64, PA-AC-BAC-128, INBACPAN016O000, INBACPAN128O000, Intesis BACnet Server Panasonic VRF
