Short answer

The DPM-C530A serves its measurements as Analog Values on BACnet MS/TP. AV 0-10 are commandable setup objects (wiring system, CT and PT ratios, demand settings, language, energy reset); AV 11-85 are read-only voltage, current, unbalance, power factor, power, energy, THD and demand values.

This page lists 86 BACnet objects for the Delta Electronics DPM-C530A power meter (BACnet MS/TP) — 11 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from a copy of Delta Electronics’s documentation hosted by BACnet International catalog, not by Delta Electronics itself, so treat it as unofficial.

BMS integration notes: Delta Electronics DPM-C530A power meter (BACnet MS/TP)

Integration in brief

Set CT/PT ratios and the wiring system at the meter (or AV 0-5) once, then read the read-only power, energy, demand, PF, current and voltage AVs.

Network setup

  • BACnet MS/TP master at 9600, 19200 or 38400 baud.
  • Device object instance and name are writable over BACnet.

Watch out for

  • AV 0-10 are configuration values (commandable); don't write them cyclically from the BMS.
  • AV 10 Reset_Parameter resets energy when written with 0x5768.

Integration notes

  • AV 11-14 are reserved.
  • Reactive and apparent energy (AV 63-66) report the unit kWh because BACnet has no kvarh / kVAh unit; the values are kvarh and kVAh.
  • The PICS prints AV 64 as 'AV_064Reactive_Energy-received' (no underscore after the number); kept as printed.
  • Device Object_Identifier and Object_Name are writable.
  • Source: BACnet International catalog copy of Delta's PICS dated 2016-01-07.

Questions about the Delta Electronics DPM-C530A power meter (BACnet MS/TP)

How many BACnet points does the Delta Electronics DPM-C530A power meter (BACnet MS/TP) have?

This list has 86 BACnet objects: 86 analog values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Delta Electronics DPM-C530A power meter (BACnet MS/TP) BACnet points are writable?

11 of the 86 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.
