DriSteem Vapor-logic 4 BACnet points list

Updated 12 November 2026

Short answer

Vapor-logic 4 is an older generation (before Vapor-logic 5 and 6) of the DriSteem humidifier control board. Modbus is standard; BACnet MS/TP (terminal P7) or BACnet IP (Ethernet) is an option bought with an unlock code. Its interoperability table lists 95 BACnet objects, plus 5 multi-tank objects in a separate table (100 in all), with underscore-style names: RH, dew point and demand readings, setpoints and fieldbus demand, run mode, digital I/O, and alarm and message flags.

This page lists 100 BACnet objects for the DriSteem Vapor-logic 4 — 69 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from DriSteem’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: DriSteem Vapor-logic 4

Integration in brief

Unlock and enable BACnet on the keypad (Setup > Communications > BACnet), set the device instance and MS/TP station or IP settings and cycle power. Then either write the space RH setpoint (AV 5) and let Vapor-logic control, or send a demand from the BMS (AV 8 / AV 9), and use MSV 1 Run_mode for auto/standby/drain.

Enable & remote start

Run_mode (MSV 1) selects Auto, Local standby, System standby or Manual drain. The humidifier also needs its safety interlock and airflow inputs made (BI 1-3 show their state).

Sensors & values from the BMS

The space, dew point and duct RH values are read-only AIs from the unit's own transmitters; the BMS can send a demand (AV 8 mass or AV 9 percent, write-only) instead.

Network setup

  • Device instance default 255; MS/TP station 1, max masters 127; default comm channel 19200 MS/TP; BACnet IP UDP port 47808.
  • BACnet is an option: enter the purchased unlock code (matched to the serial number), pick the channel, then cycle power.
  • MS/TP wiring lands on terminal P7; BACnet IP uses the Ethernet port.

Watch out for

  • Fieldbus demand objects are write-only.
  • Alarm and message BVs are listed RW but report conditions; clear faults with BV 4.
  • Many objects only apply to one humidifier type.

Integration notes

  • From Table 71-1 "Interoperability variable and object names" (object names are the "Variable name and BACnet object name" column) and Table 81-1 "Multi-tank fieldbus profiles" (names from its "BACnet description" column).
  • Where several names share an instance (BI 6 GTS/STS, BI 8-9 single-tank vs multi-tank), the names are joined with " / ". BV 7 is printed with three names (RH, dew point and demand signal out of range); it is listed under the first and the others are in its description.
  • Run_mode (MSV 1) and the AV 5-7 setpoints are printed as separate write and read rows and merged here.
  • Units follow the I-P setting (SI: °C, kg/h, kg, bar). "100 lbs" is printed for the water-until objects.
  • Multi-tank objects (AI 8-9, AV 14-15, MSV 2, and the MT names on BI 8-9) only apply in multi-tank mode.
  • Newer controllers use Vapor-logic 6, which has a different (CamelCase) object list.

Questions about the DriSteem Vapor-logic 4

How many BACnet points does the DriSteem Vapor-logic 4 have?

This list has 100 BACnet objects: 11 analog inputs, 15 analog values, 11 binary inputs, 2 binary outputs, 59 binary values, 2 multi-state values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which DriSteem Vapor-logic 4 BACnet points are writable?

69 of the 100 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official DriSteem Vapor-logic 4 BACnet documentation?

The source is Vapor-logic4 Installation and Operation Manual (VL4-IOM-0312), revision 2012 (0312), published free by DriSteem at https://d2tsmyh31fn7w8.cloudfront.net/public-documents/docs/historical-literature/vl4_iom.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the DriSteem Vapor-logic 4?

Start with MV 1 Run_mode (Run mode: 1 auto, 2/3 standby, 4 drain); AV 9 Fieldbus_demand_% (Demand from the BMS, % of capacity); AV 5 Space_RH_set_point (Space RH setpoint (unit controls)); BV 4 Clear_all_faults (Write 1 to clear faults); AI 1 Space_RH (Space RH); AV 2 Steam_output_percent (Estimated steam output, %); AI 5 Steam_demand_percent (Demand the unit is acting on, %); BV 3 Active_manually_cleared_alarms_exist (A manual-clear alarm exists).

How do I enable BMS control of the DriSteem Vapor-logic 4?

Run_mode (MSV 1) selects Auto, Local standby, System standby or Manual drain. The humidifier also needs its safety interlock and airflow inputs made (BI 1-3 show their state).

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the DriSteem Vapor-logic 4?

The space, dew point and duct RH values are read-only AIs from the unit's own transmitters; the BMS can send a demand (AV 8 mass or AV 9 percent, write-only) instead.
