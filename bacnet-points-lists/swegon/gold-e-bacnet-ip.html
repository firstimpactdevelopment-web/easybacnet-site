Short answer

The built-in IQlogic controller in Swegon GOLD generation E air handlers is a native BACnet IP device (B-AAC profile) with no gateway needed. It publishes over 1,400 objects: fan airflow/pressure readings and setpoints, temperatures and regulation levels, heat-exchanger and coil data, accessory functions (Xzone, SMART Link, AYC, MIRU, COOL DX), one binary input per alarm, plus calendars, a schedule and notification classes for intrinsic alarming. The unit is started, stopped and speed-selected from the BMS through a single multistate value.

BMS integration notes: Swegon GOLD generation E (RX/PX/CX/SD) BACnet IP

Integration in brief

Command the unit with MV-793 Communication operation level (Auto lets the internal time channel run it; other states force stop, low or high speed) and read back MI-592 Operation level plus the low/high speed BIs. Adjust the main temperature setpoints (AV-132 supply, AV-135 extract/room) and airflow or pressure setpoints for low and high speed, and monitor BI-13/14 for A/B alarms with BV-1 to reset. The object summary on page 3 of the source lists these as the key points.

Enable & remote start

MV-793 Communication operation level: 1=Auto (unit follows its own time channel), 2=Total stop, 3=Low speed, 4=High speed, 5=Normal stop, 6=Extended normal stop. MI-592 shows whether a stop came from communication, the hand terminal, a digital input, the time channel or an alarm.

Sensors & values from the BMS

Room temperature can come from the BMS by enabling BV-167 'External room temp from BMS function' and writing AV-168; outdoor temperature likewise via BV-177 and AV-178. Each has an 'alarm time' AV in minutes (AV-169, AV-179; AV-179 defaults to 5). Xzone has its own BMS room temperature (BV-379, AV-380, AV-381).

Network setup

  • BACnet IP only; the device is a B-AAC with intrinsic alarming (notification classes NC-0, 1, 3, 10, 11).
  • Swegon also publishes a BACnet PICS and an EDE file for GOLD; device instance and IP settings are not given in this document.

Watch out for

  • Calibration BVs (29-56) trigger pressure-sensor zero calibration.
  • Setpoints are split by low and high speed (e.g. AV-3/5 SA airflow, AV-19/21 SA duct pressure); write the one for the active speed.
  • Many objects only mean something when the related accessory or heat exchanger type (RX/PX/CX/SD) is installed.
  • Schedule #1 values are 1-5 (total stop to extended normal stop) with up to 6 time-values a day.

Questions about the Swegon GOLD generation E (RX/PX/CX/SD) BACnet IP

How many BACnet points does the Swegon GOLD generation E (RX/PX/CX/SD) BACnet IP have?

This list has 1452 BACnet objects: 356 analog inputs, 1 analog output, 357 analog values, 554 binary inputs, 1 binary output, 91 binary values, 2 calendars, 12 multi-state inputs, 2 multi-state outputs, 5 notification classs, 1 schedule, 70 multi-state values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Swegon GOLD generation E (RX/PX/CX/SD) BACnet IP BACnet points are writable?

525 of the 1452 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Swegon GOLD generation E (RX/PX/CX/SD) BACnet IP BACnet documentation?

The source is BACnet IP GOLD RX/PX/CX/SD, Generation E (GOLD_E_BACnet_150316), revision 2015-03-16, published free by Swegon at https://www.swegon.com/siteassets/_products-documents-archive/air-handling-units/gold-version-e-2013-2017/functions/_en/gold_e_bacnet_150316.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Swegon GOLD generation E (RX/PX/CX/SD) BACnet IP?

Start with MV 793 Communication operation level (BMS start/stop and speed command); AV 132 SA temperature set point (Supply air temperature setpoint); AV 135 EA/Room temperature set point (Extract/room temperature setpoint); BV 1 Alarm reset (Resets all active alarms); MI 592 Operation level (Actual operating state and who stopped the unit); AI 64 SA Temperature (Supply air temperature); AI 66 EA Temperature (Extract air temperature); AI 73 Outdoor temperature (Outdoor air temp measured by the unit, °C); BI 13 A-alarm active (Any A-class alarm); BI 14 B-alarm active (Any B-class alarm).

How do I enable BMS control of the Swegon GOLD generation E (RX/PX/CX/SD) BACnet IP?

MV-793 Communication operation level: 1=Auto (unit follows its own time channel), 2=Total stop, 3=Low speed, 4=High speed, 5=Normal stop, 6=Extended normal stop. MI-592 shows whether a stop came from communication, the hand terminal, a digital input, the time channel or an alarm.

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the Swegon GOLD generation E (RX/PX/CX/SD) BACnet IP?

Room temperature can come from the BMS by enabling BV-167 'External room temp from BMS function' and writing AV-168; outdoor temperature likewise via BV-177 and AV-178. Each has an 'alarm time' AV in minutes (AV-169, AV-179; AV-179 defaults to 5). Xzone has its own BMS room temperature (BV-379, AV-380, AV-381).
