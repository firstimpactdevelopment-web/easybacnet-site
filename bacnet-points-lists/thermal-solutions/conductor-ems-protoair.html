Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44) BACnet points list

Updated 12 November 2026

Short answer

Conductor EMS is the master boiler sequencer for Thermal Solutions and Bryan Steam plants. Through the ProtoAir FPA-W44 gateway it reports plant load, sequencer status and setpoint, system supply/return/outdoor temperatures, steam pressure, discrete outputs and system alarms, plus a block of 23 points for each of up to eight boilers. The BMS can enable the sequencer (BV 0) and write its setpoint (AV 0); everything else is read-only Analog Inputs, including on/off states and alarm flags.

This page lists 222 BACnet objects for the Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44) — 2 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from Thermal Solutions’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44)

Integration in brief

Cut jumper 1 on PCB-10 so BV 0 can enable the sequencer, then enable it and write the plant setpoint to AV 0. Read the active setpoint, firing rate, system temperatures, sequencer status and the per-boiler alarm flags.

Minimum points to map

The short list most integrators start with — enough to run the unit from a BMS. Map more as the job needs.

Enable & remote start

BV 0 EMS_Enable turns the master sequencer on; jumper 1 on PCB-10 must be cut first.

Network setup

  • The ProtoAir FPA-W44 gateway is set up through its web configurator; see the general sections of the same manual.

Watch out for

  • Alarm and on/off points are Analog Inputs (0/1), not Binary objects.
  • Repeat the boiler alarm mapping for each installed boiler (blocks of 21 from AI 36).

Questions about the Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44)

How many BACnet points does the Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44) have?

This list has 222 BACnet objects: 220 analog inputs, 1 analog value, 1 binary value. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44) BACnet points are writable?

2 of the 222 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44) BACnet documentation?

The source is Operating Manual, ProtoAir FPA-W44 for Interfacing Bryan Steam & Thermal Solutions Products (106417-02 Rev 13), revision 2022-10, published free by Thermal Solutions at https://www.thermalsolutions.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/106417-02-Rev-13-Thermal-Solutions-and-Bryan-ProtoAir-Manual-2022-10-Rev-13.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44)?

Start with BV 0 EMS_Enable (Enable master sequencer); AV 0 EMS_Setpoint (Sequencer setpoint, 60-230 °F); AI 2 Active_Setpoint (Setpoint in use, °F); AI 3 Status (Sequencer status code); AI 1 Firing_Rate (Plant firing rate, %); AI 7 SYSTEM_SUPPLY (System supply temperature, °F); AI 8 SYSTEM_RETURN (System return temperature, °F); AI 42 BLR1_General_Alarm (Boiler 1 general alarm (1 = fault)); AI 53 ALR_BLR1Lockout (Boiler 1 lockout (1 = fault)).

How do I enable BMS control of the Thermal Solutions Conductor EMS boiler sequencer (ProtoAir FPA-W44)?

BV 0 EMS_Enable turns the master sequencer on; jumper 1 on PCB-10 must be cut first.
