Updated 12 November 2026

Short answer

The Lincoln BMS Interface is an extension board that runs central heating (CH) and domestic hot water (DHW) control for a group of Adveco Lincoln L70 air source heat pumps and exposes it on BACnet MS/TP. The BMS can pick the CH and DHW control modes, send demand and flow or tank setpoints, run weather compensation and supplementary heating, and read the board's I/O plus inlet, outlet and ambient temperatures, compressor status, alarms and energy for each of up to 10 Lincolns.

This page lists 120 BACnet objects for the Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP — 36 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from Adveco’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP

Integration in brief

Set ch_mode_select (IV 49) to 4 (BMS Control), then switch heating with ch_bms_demand (BV 36) and set the flow temperature with ch_master_flow_SP (AV 15). For hot water set dhw_mode_select (IV 53) to 3 and use dhw_bms_demand (BV 44) with dhw_flow_SP (AV 18). Read ch_flow_SP and the fault flags.

Enable & remote start

ch_mode_select (IV 49) = 4 puts central heating under BMS control, switched by ch_bms_demand (BV 36) to the ch_master_flow_SP setpoint; dhw_mode_select (IV 53) = 3 does the same for hot water with dhw_bms_demand (BV 44).

Sensors & values from the BMS

ambient_temp_select (IV 48) picks the ambient temperature for weather compensation and low-ambient logic: the average of all active Lincolns, Lincoln 1 only, or the probe wired to the extension board.

Network setup

  • BACnet MS/TP on the BMS2 / Fieldbus 2 port; defaults device instance 1, 9600 baud (1200-375000 selectable).
  • Device instance and baud rate are set with bacnet_device_instance (PIV 118) and bacnet_baudrate (PIV 119); write Reboot_UPC (BV 120) = 1 to apply.

Watch out for

  • Weather compensation is ignored while CH runs in 4-20 mA or 0-10 V mode.

Integration notes

  • The guide gives no units; temperatures are in °C going by the prose (for example ch_master_flow_SP default 35 °C).
  • Per-heat-pump objects (instances 56-117) carry the Lincoln number as the index in the name. Temperatures, compressor status and energy are printed for Lincolns 1 to 10; the minor and major alarm objects are printed for Lincoln 1 only.
  • Access comes from the guide's Read/Write Mode column: Writeable / Read_Write = W, NoWrite = R.
  • ch_bms_demand prose says 1 = ON, 2 = OFF in one place and 0 / 1 in another; the BinaryValue object is 0 = inactive, 1 = active.
  • BACnet type IntegerValue is listed as IV and PositiveIntegerValue as PIV.

Questions about the Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP

How many BACnet points does the Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP have?

This list has 120 BACnet objects: 48 analog inputs, 17 analog values, 36 binary inputs, 9 binary values, 8 integer values, 2 positive integer values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP BACnet points are writable?

36 of the 120 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP BACnet documentation?

The source is Lincoln L70 BMS Interface BACnet Protocol, revision Rev. 1 - 07062023, published free by Adveco at https://adveco.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/L70-Lincoln-Extension-BACnet-Protocol-Rev.1.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP?

Start with IV 49 ch_mode_select (CH control mode; 4 = BMS Control); BV 36 ch_bms_demand (CH demand from the BMS (1 = active)); IV 53 dhw_mode_select (DHW control mode; 3 = BMS Control); BV 44 dhw_bms_demand (DHW demand from the BMS); AV 15 ch_master_flow_SP (CH flow setpoint in DI/BMS mode, °C); AV 18 dhw_flow_SP (DHW flow setpoint, °C); AI 13 ch_flow_SP (CH flow setpoint in use); BI 37 ch_demand (CH demand active); BI 39 ges_major_fault (Any major fault); BI 40 ges_minor_fault (Any minor fault).

How do I enable BMS control of the Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP?

ch_mode_select (IV 49) = 4 puts central heating under BMS control, switched by ch_bms_demand (BV 36) to the ch_master_flow_SP setpoint; dhw_mode_select (IV 53) = 3 does the same for hot water with dhw_bms_demand (BV 44).

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the Adveco Lincoln L70 BMS Interface (extension board) BACnet MS/TP?

ambient_temp_select (IV 48) picks the ambient temperature for weather compensation and low-ambient logic: the average of all active Lincolns, Lincoln 1 only, or the probe wired to the extension board.
