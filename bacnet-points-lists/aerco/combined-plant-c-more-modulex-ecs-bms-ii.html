Short answer

AERCO's ProtoNode control-points workbook has a combined profile for a mixed plant: 12 C-More boilers (9 objects each), 4 Modulex boilers (10 each), 6 ECS/SmartPlate valves (7 each) and two BMS II managers (29 objects for the C-More plant, 13 for the Modulex plant), 232 Analog Values in all. Most are read-only; setpoints and Modulex direct-drive commands are writable.

BMS integration notes: AERCO Combined plant: 12 C-More, 4 Modulex, 6 ECS/SmartPlate, 2 BMS II (ProtoNode points list)

Integration in brief

Write the header setpoints to the two BMS II objects (setpt_128 for the C-More plant, setpt_228 for the Modulex plant), or per-unit setpoints and Modulex direct-drive commands where the plant is run unit by unit. Read header temperatures, boilers fired/online, each unit's fire rate, outlet temperature, status and display/error codes.

Enable & remote start

The sheet marks setpt_128 / setpt_228 (network header setpoints), each C-More setpt_n (net remote setpoint), each Modulex setpt_n and boilercmd_n (requested outlet temperature and net direct drive) and each ECS rmsetpt_n as writable. It does not describe the BMS II or C-More modes needed for these to take effect.

Network setup

  • Device addresses in this profile: C-More 1-12, Modulex 13-16, ECS/SmartPlate valves 17-22, BMS II 128 (C-More plant) and 228 (Modulex plant).
  • The sheet title lists BACnet IP, BACnet MS/TP, Metasys N2 and LonWorks; it gives no device instance or network settings.

Watch out for

  • Object IDs follow this combined profile only; the single-plant profiles (4/8/12 C-More, Modulex, ECS) use other IDs.
  • Many objects share the same AERCO point label (01-Fire Rate Out ...); the unit is in the description and in the object name suffix.
  • Units and ranges are not given in the sheet.

Questions about the AERCO Combined plant: 12 C-More, 4 Modulex, 6 ECS/SmartPlate, 2 BMS II (ProtoNode points list)

How many BACnet points does the AERCO Combined plant: 12 C-More, 4 Modulex, 6 ECS/SmartPlate, 2 BMS II (ProtoNode points list) have?

This list has 232 BACnet objects: 232 analog values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which AERCO Combined plant: 12 C-More, 4 Modulex, 6 ECS/SmartPlate, 2 BMS II (ProtoNode points list) BACnet points are writable?

28 of the 232 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official AERCO Combined plant: 12 C-More, 4 Modulex, 6 ECS/SmartPlate, 2 BMS II (ProtoNode points list) BACnet documentation?

The source is AERCO ProtoNode Control Points (BACnet/IP, BACnet MS/TP, Metasys N2 and LonWorks Points List), sheet 12 C-Mores, 6 ECS/SP, 4 Modulex and 2 BMS II, published free by AERCO at https://www.watts.com/dfsmedia/0533dbba17714b1ab581ab07a4cbb521/57673-source/AERCO-ProtoNode-Control-Points.xlsx.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the AERCO Combined plant: 12 C-More, 4 Modulex, 6 ECS/SmartPlate, 2 BMS II (ProtoNode points list)?

Start with AV 262 setpt_128 (C-More plant header setpoint from the BMS); AV 302 setpt_228 (Modulex plant header setpoint from the BMS); AV 263 localsuptemp_128 (C-More plant header temperature); AV 303 localsuptemp_228 (Modulex plant header temperature); AV 266 blrfired_128 (Number of C-More boilers firing); AV 1 boilerstate_1 (C-More boiler 1 fire rate (repeat per boiler)); AV 8 unitstat_1 (C-More boiler 1 unit status (repeat per boiler)); AV 265 dispcode_128 (C-More BMS II display (fault) code); AV 128 errcode_13 (Modulex 13 error code (repeat per unit)).

How do I enable BMS control of the AERCO Combined plant: 12 C-More, 4 Modulex, 6 ECS/SmartPlate, 2 BMS II (ProtoNode points list)?

The sheet marks setpt_128 / setpt_228 (network header setpoints), each C-More setpt_n (net remote setpoint), each Modulex setpt_n and boilercmd_n (requested outlet temperature and net direct drive) and each ECS rmsetpt_n as writable. It does not describe the BMS II or C-More modes needed for these to take effect.
