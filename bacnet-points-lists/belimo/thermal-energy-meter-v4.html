Belimo Thermal Energy Meter V4 BACnet points list

Updated 16 October 2026

Short answer

The Belimo Thermal Energy Meter combines an ultrasonic flow sensor with supply and return temperature sensors to measure heating and cooling power and energy in hydronic circuits. It offers BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP (with foreign-device registration). It is a read-only metering device apart from unit selection, meter-register choice and the extra sensor input settings.

This page lists 35 BACnet objects for the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter V4 — 8 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from Belimo’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: Belimo Thermal Energy Meter V4

Integration in brief

Set the units once (MV 123-127), then read heating and cooling power and energy, flow and the two temperatures. Use the PIV energy objects if the BMS prefers integer totals. Watch MV 99 and AV 140 for faults.

Network setup

  • Default device instance 1.
  • MS/TP: baud 9600-115200, default 38400; MAC address 0-127, default 1; max 32 nodes without repeater.
  • BACnet IP: UDP port configurable, default 47808; foreign-device registration supported.
  • Configure with Belimo Assistant 2 or the built-in web server; bus termination shows in BV 99.

Watch out for

  • Writable objects with instance 90 and above are persistent; do not write them on a regular basis.
  • Avoid toggling MV 200 between certified and lifetime registers, it disrupts energy logging.
  • The certified register resets when the sensor module is replaced.
  • Max 6 active COV subscriptions.

Integration notes

  • The document prints access as R / W; those objects are listed here as writable (W).
  • Units of flow, power, energy, volume and temperature objects follow the unit-select MVs 123-127.
  • MV 200 picks the certified register (MID models 22PEM only) or the lifetime register; energy and volume objects follow it.
  • Sensor 1 objects (AI 20/21, AV 20, BI 20) are out of service unless MV 220/221 select the matching sensor type.
  • Value ranges depend on pipe size (DN).

Questions about the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter V4

How many BACnet points does the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter V4 have?

This list has 35 BACnet objects: 4 analog inputs, 12 analog values, 1 binary input, 1 binary value, 12 multi-state values, 5 positive integer values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Belimo Thermal Energy Meter V4 BACnet points are writable?

8 of the 35 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Belimo Thermal Energy Meter V4 BACnet documentation?

The source is BACnet Interface Description, Thermal Energy Meter (TEM), DN 15...50 (Version 4), revision 2025-11 / V4.2.0, published free by Belimo at https://www.belimo.com/mam/general-documents/system_integration/BACnet/belimo_BACnet_Interface-description_TEM_V4_2_en-gb.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter V4?

Start with AV 48 HeatingEnergy_UnitSel (Heating energy total); AV 47 CoolingEnergy_UnitSel (Cooling energy total); AV 46 HeatingPower_UnitSel (Heating power delivered; units set by MV 124); AV 45 CoolingPower_UnitSel (Cooling power delivered; units set by MV 124); AV 19 AbsFlow_UnitSel (Measured flow); AI 22 T1_UnitSel (Remote temperature); AI 23 T2_UnitSel (Flow body temperature); MV 99 SummaryStatus (Overall OK / warning / not OK); AV 140 ErrorState (Bit-coded fault and warning flags).

How do I check these points on the real Thermal Energy Meter V4?

Scan the network with a BACnet IP browser such as Easy BACnet, open the device and compare its object list with this table. Anything missing is usually an option that isn't installed or a point that is disabled in the controller's configuration.
