Belimo Energy Valve V4 BACnet points list

Updated 16 October 2026

Short answer

The Energy Valve V4 is a pressure-independent characterized control valve with an ultrasonic flow sensor and two temperature sensors, so it reports flow, delta T, power and heating/cooling energy. It speaks BACnet MS/TP and BACnet IP (including foreign-device registration). The setpoint on AO 1 means position, flow, power or differential pressure depending on the control mode in MV 100, and the engineering units of most analog objects follow selectable unit objects.

This page lists 68 BACnet objects for the Belimo Energy Valve V4 — 27 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from Belimo’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: Belimo Energy Valve V4

Integration in brief

Set MV 122 SpSource to Bus, choose the control mode in MV 100 and the flow and power limits, then command AO 1 SpRel from the BMS. Read flow, temperatures, delta T, power and energy for monitoring, and watch MV 99 SummaryStatus and AV 140 ErrorState for faults. If you enable a bus fail action, the BMS must keep rewriting the setpoint inside the watchdog time.

Enable & remote start

Write MV 122 SpSource = 2 (Bus) so the valve follows AO 1 SpRel instead of the 0-10 V signal on wire 3. MV 1 Override can force open, close, min, max, nominal or motor stop.

Heartbeat & communication loss

AV 130 BusWatchdog sets a timeout of 5-3600 s (default 120 s). If no write arrives within that time, the valve runs the action in MV 130 BusFailAction (default None; options Open, Close, Max, Min, Stop) and sets the bus watchdog bit in AV 140. With a fail action set, the BMS must keep rewriting the setpoint.

Network setup

  • Default device instance 1; on MS/TP the device ID is this value plus the MAC address.
  • MS/TP: baud 9600-115200, default 38400; MAC address 0-127, default 1; max 32 nodes per segment without repeater.
  • BACnet IP: UDP port configurable, default 47808; foreign-device registration supported.
  • Configure with Belimo Assistant 2 or the valve's built-in web server.
  • Bus termination (120 Ω) is set with Belimo Assistant 2; BV 99 shows its state.

Watch out for

  • Writable objects with instance 90 and above are stored persistently; do not write them on a regular schedule.
  • Changing MV 100 ControlMode resets the valve.
  • Engineering units change when the unit-select MVs are written; recheck BMS scaling after any change.
  • Vmax below 25% of nominal flow is raised to 25%.
  • Avoid switching MV 200 between certified and lifetime registers, it breaks energy trending.
  • Max 6 active COV subscriptions.

Integration notes

  • Version 1-3 Energy Valves use a different object list; this file covers V4 only.
  • Units of flow, power, energy, volume, temperature and pressure objects follow the unit-select MVs 123-131 and can change.
  • MV 200 selects the certified (MID models only) or lifetime meter register for the energy and volume objects.
  • Sensor 1 objects (AI 20/21, AV 20, BI 20) are out of service unless MV 220/221 select the matching sensor type.
  • Differential pressure objects are used with the differential pressure control mode.
  • AV 55 and AV 135 are both printed as AbsDiffPressure in the document; check the names read from the device.

Questions about the Belimo Energy Valve V4

How many BACnet points does the Belimo Energy Valve V4 have?

This list has 68 BACnet objects: 6 analog inputs, 1 analog output, 29 analog values, 1 binary input, 1 binary value, 25 multi-state values, 5 positive integer values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Belimo Energy Valve V4 BACnet points are writable?

27 of the 68 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Belimo Energy Valve V4 BACnet documentation?

The source is BACnet Interface Description, Energy Valve DN 15...50 (Version 4), revision 2024-09 / V4.2.0, published free by Belimo at https://www.belimo.com/mam/general-documents/system_integration/BACnet/belimo_BACnet_Interface-description_Energy-Valve_V4_2_en-gb.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Belimo Energy Valve V4?

Start with AO 1 SpRel (Setpoint from the BMS (position, flow, power or dp, per control mode)); MV 122 SpSource (Must be Bus for AO 1 to take effect); MV 100 ControlMode (Selects what the setpoint means); AV 19 AbsFlow_UnitSel (Measured flow); AV 22 DeltaT_UnitSel (Coil delta T); AV 46 HeatingPower_UnitSel (Heating power); AV 45 CoolingPower_UnitSel (Cooling power); AV 47 CoolingEnergy_UnitSel (Cooling energy meter); AV 48 HeatingEnergy_UnitSel (Heating energy meter); MV 99 SummaryStatus (Overall OK / warning / not OK).

How do I enable BMS control of the Belimo Energy Valve V4?

Write MV 122 SpSource = 2 (Bus) so the valve follows AO 1 SpRel instead of the 0-10 V signal on wire 3. MV 1 Override can force open, close, min, max, nominal or motor stop.

Does the Belimo Energy Valve V4 need a BACnet heartbeat or have a communication timeout?

AV 130 BusWatchdog sets a timeout of 5-3600 s (default 120 s). If no write arrives within that time, the valve runs the action in MV 130 BusFailAction (default None; options Open, Close, Max, Min, Stop) and sets the bus watchdog bit in AV 140. With a fail action set, the BMS must keep rewriting the setpoint.
