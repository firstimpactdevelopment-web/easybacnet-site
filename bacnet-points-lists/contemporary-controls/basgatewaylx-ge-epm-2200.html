Network setup

  • The BASgatewayLX presents each Modbus device as its own virtual BACnet device on BACnet IP; give it a unique device instance and Modbus address (serial) or IP address (Modbus TCP) when you add it with this profile.
  • One BASgatewayLX handles up to 100 Modbus devices and 1000 polled points in total, so leave unneeded rows unpolled.

Watch out for

  • Reset Energy Accumulator (AO 107) clears the energy totals; leave it unpolled.
  • Unpolled rows do not appear as BACnet objects until enabled in the gateway's device profile page.

Integration notes

  • Built from Contemporary Controls' BASgatewayLX Modbus device profile GE_EPM2200_Meter_a_LX.csv; the profile maps the registers in the maker's Modbus register document (https://www.ccontrols.com/support/dp/EPM2200.pdf).
  • Object instances are the ones this profile assigns when it is loaded into the gateway. A site that edited or rebuilt the profile may use different instances.
  • Only 37 of the 137 rows are flagged for polling in the profile; the rest are served only after you tick them in the gateway. Their descriptions say "not polled by default".
  • The CSV has no read/write column: Analog/Binary Inputs are read-only and Analog/Binary Outputs are commandable (priority array), which is how the gateway maps them (access inferred). Descriptions give the Modbus register each object reads.
  • Some names appear more than once in the profile as published (Watts 3-Ph total *; VARs 3-Ph total *; VAs 3-Ph total *...); they are kept as printed and told apart by instance.

Questions about the Contemporary Controls GE EPM 2200 via BASgatewayLX

How many BACnet points does the Contemporary Controls GE EPM 2200 via BASgatewayLX have?

This list has 137 BACnet objects: 136 analog inputs, 1 analog output. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Contemporary Controls GE EPM 2200 via BASgatewayLX BACnet points are writable?

1 of the 137 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done. The source has no read/write column, so this is inferred from object type.

Where is the official Contemporary Controls GE EPM 2200 via BASgatewayLX BACnet documentation?

The source is BASgatewayLX Modbus device profile GE_EPM2200_Meter_a (GE_EPM2200_Meter_a_LX.csv), published free by Contemporary Controls at https://www.ccontrols.com/support/dp/GE_EPM2200_LX_File.zip.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Contemporary Controls GE EPM 2200 via BASgatewayLX?

Start with AI 1 Watts 3-Ph total (Total watts (3-phase)); AI 20 W-hours Delivered (Delivered energy (Wh)); AI 19 W-hours Received (Received energy (Wh)); AI 7 Volts A-B (Voltage A-B); AI 10 Amps A (Current A); AI 16 Power Factor 3-Ph total (Total power factor); AI 17 Frequency (Frequency).
