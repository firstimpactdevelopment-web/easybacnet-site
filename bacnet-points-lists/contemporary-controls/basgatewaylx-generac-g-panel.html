Short answer

Gateway profile for the Generac G-Panel: 197 objects (45 AI, 32 BI, 120 BO), 151 polled by default. Analog inputs give engine temperatures and pressures, fuel and coolant level, battery, bus and generator voltages, currents, kW, PF, frequency and rpm. The panel's status and alarm flags (common alarm, running, in auto, ready to accept load, each shutdown and warning) are mapped as Binary Outputs because they are Modbus coils.

Network setup

  • The BASgatewayLX presents each Modbus device as its own virtual BACnet device on BACnet IP; give it a unique device instance and Modbus address (serial) or IP address (Modbus TCP) when you add it with this profile.
  • One BASgatewayLX handles up to 100 Modbus devices and 1000 polled points in total, so leave unneeded rows unpolled.

Watch out for

  • Status and alarm flags are Binary Outputs (coils) in this profile; treat them as read-only and never write them.
  • Unpolled rows do not appear as BACnet objects until enabled in the gateway's device profile page.
