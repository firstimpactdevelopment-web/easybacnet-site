Kemper KTS BACnet Gateway Fig. 916 02 022 (KTS hot water stations) BACnet points list

Updated 12 November 2026

Short answer

The KTS BACnet Gateway connects Kemper KTS fresh-water (potable hot water) stations to a BMS over BACnet IP; it also offers Modbus RTU. It exposes the system's thermal disinfection controls, the hot water setpoint and device clock, buffer and circulation temperatures, and for each of up to 8 cascaded stations the temperatures, flow, primary pump output, faults and operating mode.

BMS integration notes: Kemper KTS BACnet Gateway Fig. 916 02 022 (KTS hot water stations)

Integration in brief

Map the station operating mode (BV x27), hot water setpoint (AV 100) and the per-station temperatures, then the common fault and coded fault per station. Thermal disinfection is started and cancelled through BV 5, with its status on MI 6. Scale temperatures by 0.1.

Enable & remote start

Each station's BV x27 Betriebsart sets it to off (0) or automatic (1).

Network setup

  • Physical interfaces: Ethernet (BACnet IP) and RS485 (Modbus RTU, 9600 / 8 / N / 1).
  • The device clock can be set through AV 101-106.

Watch out for

  • Values are scaled integers: divide temperatures and flow by 10, firmware version by 100.
  • Thermal disinfection points only read once the function is enabled in the controller.
  • The station 2 relay numbering (R1, R2) differs from the single-unit block (R3, R4).

Integration notes

  • Point list is in German; object names are kept as printed and descriptions are English.
  • Per-station objects are printed as ranges, for example AI-108 … AI-808: the hundreds digit is the station number (1-8). Only the station 1 instances are listed; add 100 per station.
  • Temperatures and the setpoints are sent as °C x10 and flow as l/min x10.
  • BI 13-AI 33 are for a single (non-cascade) unit and BI 213-AI 231 for station 2, as printed.
  • Thermal disinfection must be enabled in the controller, otherwise AV 2-MI 6 cannot be read.

Questions about the Kemper KTS BACnet Gateway Fig. 916 02 022 (KTS hot water stations)

How many BACnet points does the Kemper KTS BACnet Gateway Fig. 916 02 022 (KTS hot water stations) have?

This list has 46 BACnet objects: 21 analog inputs, 10 analog values, 12 binary inputs, 2 binary values, 1 multi-state input. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Kemper KTS BACnet Gateway Fig. 916 02 022 (KTS hot water stations) BACnet points are writable?

13 of the 46 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Kemper KTS BACnet Gateway Fig. 916 02 022 (KTS hot water stations) BACnet documentation?

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Kemper KTS BACnet Gateway Fig. 916 02 022 (KTS hot water stations)?

Start with BV 127 Betriebsart (Station 1 mode, 0 = off, 1 = auto (BV 227-827 others)); AV 100 Temperatur PWH Soll (Hot water setpoint, °C x10); BV 5 Starten / abbrechen (Thermal disinfection, 1 = start, 0 = cancel); AI 109 S2 - PWH Temperatur (Station 1 hot water outlet temp, °C x10); AI 117 V - PWH Volumenstrom (Station 1 hot water flow, l/min x10); MI 6 Status (Thermal disinfection status); BI 125 Sammelstörung (Station 1 common fault; write 0 to acknowledge); AI 126 Codierte Fehlermeldung (Station 1 bit-coded fault messages); BI 1 Kommunikationsstörung zum Regler (Gateway lost communication with the controller).
