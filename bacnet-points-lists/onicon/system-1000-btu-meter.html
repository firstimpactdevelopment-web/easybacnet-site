ONICON System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System BACnet points list

Updated 6 November 2026

Short answer

The System-1000 is a one- or two-channel BTU meter with native BACnet MS/TP or BACnet IP. Per channel it exposes energy rate, flow, supply, return and delta temperature, an aux input and COP as Analog Inputs, and energy, volume and aux totals (non-resettable, year-to-date, previous year and resettable user totals) as Analog Values. Binary Inputs give mode, alarms and a system fault.

This page lists 83 BACnet objects for the ONICON System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System — 14 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from ONICON’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: ONICON System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System

Integration in brief

Set the MS/TP address (default 128 is a slave address, so it must be changed to 0-127) or the IP settings from the meter menu, then power-cycle. Read energy rate, flow, temperatures and the energy totals for each channel, plus the alarm and fault BIs.

Network setup

  • Menu: User Configurations > Network > BACnet MS/TP or BACnet UDP/IP.
  • MS/TP defaults: 38400 baud, address 128 (slave, set 0-127 to communicate), device instance 57017, Max Master 127.
  • BACnet IP defaults: IP 192.168.1.24, mask 255.255.255.0, UDP port 47808, instance 57017.
  • Power-cycle the meter after any network change.

Watch out for

  • The default MS/TP address 128 is a slave address; the meter will not join the token ring until it is set to 0-127.
  • Two objects are printed as AI 6; check which instance holds Delta Temp Ch. 1.
  • Only the User totals reset (write 0); the other totals are non-resettable.

Integration notes

  • The object list prints 'AI 6' twice: once for Delta Temp Ch. 1 and once for AUX Input Ch. 1. Delta Temp Ch. 1 is listed here as AI 5, the gap in the sequence; confirm on the meter.
  • The table gives no units. Defaults shown are typical US units; energy, flow and temperature units are set on the meter.
  • Channel 2 objects (AI 8-15, AV 20-39, BI 2-3) carry data only on two-channel models. AI 16 (net rate between channels) is used only when configured.
  • Access is not in the object list. The PICS says the 'user defined' and aux pulse totals can be reset by writing 0; the resettable User totals are marked W here, all else R.
  • BI 2 is printed with the description 'current flow direction' and BI 3 with the mode text; the names suggest the descriptions are swapped by one row.
  • BI 8 is not listed.

Questions about the ONICON System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System

How many BACnet points does the ONICON System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System have?

This list has 83 BACnet objects: 17 analog inputs, 57 analog values, 9 binary inputs. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which ONICON System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System BACnet points are writable?

14 of the 83 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done. The source has no read/write column, so this is inferred from object type.

Where is the official ONICON System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System BACnet documentation?

The source is System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System Installation and Operation Guide (DOC-0008270 Rev.B), published free by ONICON at https://www.onicon.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/System-1000-manual-DOC-0008270.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the ONICON System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System?

Start with AI 0 Energy Rate Ch. 1 (Channel 1 thermal energy rate); AI 1 Volume Rate Ch. 1 (Channel 1 flow rate); AI 3 Supply Temp Ch. 1 (Channel 1 supply temperature); AI 4 Return Temp Ch. 1 (Channel 1 return temperature); AV 0 Energy Total Mode 1 Ch. 1 (Channel 1 energy total, non-resettable (mode 1)); AV 8 Volume Total Mode 1 Ch. 1 (Channel 1 volume total, non-resettable (mode 1)); BI 0 Mode Indication Ch. 1 (Channel 1 mode: cooling/forward or heating/reverse); BI 1 Alarm Ch. 1 (Channel 1 reading outside its limits); BI 9 System Fault (Meter system error).

How do I check these points on the real System-1000 Flow and Energy Measurement System?

Scan the network with a BACnet IP browser such as Easy BACnet, open the device and compare its object list with this table. Anything missing is usually an option that isn't installed or a point that is disabled in the controller's configuration.
