Short answer

The PIC-SD is the single-duct VAV member of Price's PIC family, with BACnet MS/TP and VAV airflow sensing built in. Its object list is shorter than the PIC's (two analog and two binary outputs, two spare voltage inputs). Instance numbers differ from the PIC for the same functions, so don't reuse a PIC template unchanged.

BMS integration notes: Price Industries PIC-SD Price Intelligent Controller - SD

Integration in brief

Set a unique MAC and device instance at the thermostat, set the segment baud (Price default 76800), then map the occupancy override, room setpoint, room temperature, airflow and the alarm-level MVs. The controller runs its own airflow and reheat sequence; the BMS mostly adjusts setpoints and occupancy.

Enable & remote start

No enable point. Occupancy comes from airflow, a contact closure, the thermostat button or a motion sensor; the BMS can force it with MV 4 Occupancy Override (1 = No Override, 2 = Force Unoccupied, 3 = Force Occupied). MV 3 shows what set the current occupancy.

Network setup

  • BACnet MS/TP is built into the PIC-SD.
  • Price's PIC manual gives 76800 as the default baud for Price products (9600, 19200 and 38400 also available). All devices on a segment must match.
  • MAC address (AV 30, default 1, range 1-127) and device instance (AV 29, default 101) are writable objects.
  • MV 14 MAC/DI Link (default Linked) ties the device instance to the MAC address.

Watch out for

  • Alarm MVs report a level (1-4) or 5 = No Alarm; levels follow the AV alarm-threshold settings.
  • Room setpoint writes are limited by the high/low limit AVs.
  • Temperature objects follow the Temperature Units setting (°F or °C).

Integration notes

  • The source prints AO1 and AO2 a second time against Airflow Target and Room Setpoint - High Limit. AO 1/AO 2 are already the analog outputs, the setpoint ranges refer to AV2/AV3 and the PIC lists the same points as AV 1/AV 2, so they are listed here as AV 1 and AV 2. Check on the device.
  • Only one low-temperature alarm threshold is listed (AV 24, Level 2); there is no Level 3 low-temperature threshold object.
  • MV 2 Occupancy uses 1 = Occupied, 2 = Unoccupied, the reverse of the PIC.
  • AI 6 and AI 7 (named AI1 and AI2) are the spare voltage inputs.
  • Same family as the PIC (Price Intelligent Controller) but a separate, shorter object list.

Questions about the Price Industries PIC-SD Price Intelligent Controller - SD

How many BACnet points does the Price Industries PIC-SD Price Intelligent Controller - SD have?

This list has 55 BACnet objects: 7 analog inputs, 3 analog outputs, 28 analog values, 1 binary input, 2 binary outputs, 14 multi-state values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Price Industries PIC-SD Price Intelligent Controller - SD BACnet points are writable?

41 of the 55 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Price Industries PIC-SD Price Intelligent Controller - SD BACnet documentation?

The source is BACnet Points List - PIC-SD, revision REV 1, published free by Price Industries at https://legacy.priceindustries.com/content/uploads/assets/literature/documents---bacnet/pic-sd---bacnet-points-list.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Price Industries PIC-SD Price Intelligent Controller - SD?

Start with MV 4 Occupancy Override (Network occupancy: 2 = force unocc, 3 = force occ); AV 6 Room Setpoint (Occupied room setpoint); MV 2 Occupancy (Current occupancy (1 = Occupied, 2 = Unoccupied)); MV 7 Controller State (Cooling, heating or deadband); AI 1 Room Temperature (Room temperature); AI 2 Airflow (Measured airflow); AV 1 Airflow Target (Airflow target being controlled to); MV 8 High Temperature Alarm (High room temp alarm level (5 = no alarm)); MV 9 Low Temperature Alarm (Low room temp alarm level (5 = no alarm)); MV 10 Low Airflow Alarm (Low airflow alarm level (5 = no alarm)).

How do I enable BMS control of the Price Industries PIC-SD Price Intelligent Controller - SD?

No enable point. Occupancy comes from airflow, a contact closure, the thermostat button or a motion sensor; the BMS can force it with MV 4 Occupancy Override (1 = No Override, 2 = Force Unoccupied, 3 = Force Occupied). MV 3 shows what set the current occupancy.
