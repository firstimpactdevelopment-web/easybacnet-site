EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC) BACnet points list

Updated 12 November 2026

Short answer

The Sage PLC panel runs EVAPCO fluid cooler cells: pumps, fans on VFDs, make-up water, basin heaters, louver de-icing and lead/lag between cells. It talks BACnet MS/TP on its RS-485 port (or Modbus RTU, chosen at the HMI) and BACnet IP on its Ethernet port. The BMS can enable the system, send a leaving fluid setpoint, acknowledge alarms and must toggle a heartbeat object.

This page lists 164 BACnet objects for the EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC) — 25 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from EVAPCO’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC)

Integration in brief

Pick BACnet MS/TP or Modbus RTU for the serial port at the HMI (BACnet IP uses the Ethernet port; IP and MS/TP are not available together). Write bnEnableSystem to enable the system and bnBASSP for the leaving fluid setpoint, and keep the BMS heartbeat toggling. Monitor bnSYSEnabled, bnLocalRemoteStatus, the common leaving temperature, cell fan speeds and the alarm flags; bnALM_ACK acknowledges alarms.

Enable & remote start

bnEnableSystem (BV 35) enables the system from the BMS; bnLocalRemoteStatus shows whether the panel is in local or remote control.

Heartbeat & communication loss

The guide lists bnBMSHeartbeat: 0 means waiting for the PLC to set it to 1 (5 s), 1 means waiting for the BMS to write it back to 0 (30 s). The guide does not say what the panel does when the BMS misses it. It is printed with the same instance as another object (BV 109), so confirm the live object before mapping it.

Network setup

  • Default communication parameters are printed on the Network Wiring page of the panel wiring schematic.
  • The serial protocol (BACnet MS/TP or Modbus RTU) is chosen in the HMI Operator Menu.
  • Modbus TCP stays active on the Ethernet port alongside BACnet IP.

Watch out for

  • Do not write setpoints or other non-volatile objects cyclically; the memory is rated for about 100,000 writes.
  • Runtime reset objects (pump/fan runtime reset) are writable and clear the hour counters.
  • Temperatures are tenths of a degree F.

Integration notes

  • Objects are numbered for cells 1-4; objects for cells that are not installed have no meaning. Cell 1 and cell 2 have more objects than cells 3 and 4.
  • Temperatures are in 0.1 °F (850 = 85.0 °F) and fan speeds in 0.1 %.
  • The guide prints BINARY_VALUE 109 twice: bnC4DischargeDamper (cell 4 damper status) and bnBMSHeartbeat. Only the first is listed here; check which one the panel serves before mapping the heartbeat.
  • The guide warns that non-volatile objects (setpoints, configuration) are rated for about 100,000 writes; do not write them cyclically.

Questions about the EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC)

How many BACnet points does the EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC) have?

This list has 164 BACnet objects: 54 analog values, 110 binary values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC) BACnet points are writable?

25 of the 164 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC) BACnet documentation?

The source is Operations & Maintenance Instructions Addendum - Communications Guide for Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System, revision Revision 1.3, published free by EVAPCO at https://www.evapco.com/media/1962586.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC)?

Start with BV 35 bnEnableSystem (System enable from the BMS (1 = enabled)); AV 24 bnBASSP (Leaving fluid setpoint, 0.1 °F (850 = 85.0 °F)); BV 36 bnALM_ACK (Acknowledge alarms (1 = acknowledge)); BV 25 bnLocalRemoteStatus (Control mode: 0 local, 1 remote); BV 26 bnSYSEnabled (System enabled status); AV 52 bnActiveSetpoint (Active fluid setpoint in use, 0.1 °F); AV 30 bnCommonLeavingTemp (Average leaving fluid temperature, 0.1 °F); AV 28 bnC1VFDSpeed (Cell 1 fan speed, 0.1 %); BV 21 bnC1VFDFaultALM (Cell 1 VFD fault alarm); BV 103 bnHighCommonTempALM (High common supply water temperature alarm).

How do I enable BMS control of the EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC)?

bnEnableSystem (BV 35) enables the system from the BMS; bnLocalRemoteStatus shows whether the panel is in local or remote control.

Does the EVAPCO Sage Water & Energy Conservation Control System (Sage PLC) need a BACnet heartbeat or have a communication timeout?

The guide lists bnBMSHeartbeat: 0 means waiting for the PLC to set it to 1 (5 s), 1 means waiting for the BMS to write it back to 0 (30 s). The guide does not say what the panel does when the BMS misses it. It is printed with the same instance as another object (BV 109), so confirm the live object before mapping it.
