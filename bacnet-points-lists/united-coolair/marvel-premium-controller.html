BMS integration notes: United CoolAir Marvel Premium controller (BMS points list)

Integration in brief

Start and stop the unit with BV 1 SystemOnOff or drive modes with MV 1 BMS_ModeSelection, write the setpoint for the control type in use (return, space or supply), and read temperatures, MV 7 SystemStatus, blower status and alarms. BV 141 resets active alarms.

Enable & remote start

BV 1 SystemOnOff (R/W) switches the unit; MV 1 BMS_ModeSelection (R/W) selects 1 disabled, 2 unit off, 3 morning warm-up, 4 cool-down, 5 setback, 6 unoccupied, 7 occupied, 8 emergency stop. Per the IOM, BMS-based unoccupied override, morning warm-up and cool-down need the BMS communications option.

Network setup

  • The IOM shows BMS communications on the J25 RS-485 port (BACnet or Modbus) and pCOWeb as options.

Watch out for

  • Schedule PIVs (start/end hour and minute per day) are configuration; avoid cyclic writes.
  • Alarm flags are BVs with Normal/Alarm texts; check each alarm the unit options use.

Questions about the United CoolAir Marvel Premium controller (BMS points list)

How many BACnet points does the United CoolAir Marvel Premium controller (BMS points list) have?

This list has 526 BACnet objects: 206 analog values, 140 binary values, 9 multi-state values, 8 integer values, 163 positive integer values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which United CoolAir Marvel Premium controller (BMS points list) BACnet points are writable?

223 of the 526 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official United CoolAir Marvel Premium controller (BMS points list) BACnet documentation?

The source is BACNET Form MARVPREM_BMS_07_14_2023 (Marvel Premium BMS points list) (MARVPREM_BMS_07_14_2023 Rev. G), revision 2023-07-14, published free by United CoolAir at https://www.unitedcoolair.com/wp-content/uploads/securepdfs/MARVPREM_BMS_7_14_23.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the United CoolAir Marvel Premium controller (BMS points list)?

Start with BV 1 SystemOnOff (Unit on/off command); MV 1 BMS_ModeSelection (BMS mode: occupied, unoccupied, setback, off); AV 65 SpaceTemp_Setpoint (Space temperature setpoint, default 73); AV 56 ReturnTemp_Setpoint (Return temperature setpoint, default 72); BV 141 AlarmReset_BMS (Reset active alarms); MV 7 SystemStatus (Unit state (off, on, unoccupied, cooling...)); BV 3 SupplyBlower (Supply blower status); AV 3 SpaceTemperature (Space temperature); AV 1 ReturnTemperature (Return air temperature); AV 7 SupplyTemperature (Supply air temperature (average)).

How do I enable BMS control of the United CoolAir Marvel Premium controller (BMS points list)?

BV 1 SystemOnOff (R/W) switches the unit; MV 1 BMS_ModeSelection (R/W) selects 1 disabled, 2 unit off, 3 morning warm-up, 4 cool-down, 5 setback, 6 unoccupied, 7 occupied, 8 emergency stop. Per the IOM, BMS-based unoccupied override, morning warm-up and cool-down need the BMS communications option.
