Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102) BACnet points list

Updated 16 October 2026

Short answer

The MCA 109 option card adds a fuller BACnet MS/TP interface to the FC-102 drive used on Bell & Gossett pumps. Besides drive I/O, commands and readings, it exposes three extended PID controllers and individual binary values for every drive warning and alarm, which makes fault reporting to the BAS much more detailed than the embedded interface.

This page lists 297 BACnet objects for the Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102) — 81 of them writable — with each object’s type, instance number, name, engineering units and access. It is re-typed from Bell & Gossett’s own free documentation, linked below.

BMS integration notes: Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102)

Integration in brief

The MCA 109 option card list gives start, coast, direction, jog and reset commands, references and setpoints, three extended PID controllers, and one binary value per drive warning and alarm. A typical integration writes BV 1 and a reference or setpoint, then reads status, speed, power and the warning/alarm bits it cares about.

The short list most integrators start with — enough to run the unit from a BMS. Map more as the job needs.

Enable & remote start

BV 1 Start, BV 2 Coasting, BV 3 CW/CCW and BV 4 Jog are writable commands, and BV 13 shows whether the drive is under bus control. The point list does not explain how bus control is set up on the drive.

Sensors & values from the BMS

AV 2-4 Bus Feedback1-3 and the Ext. 1-3 setpoint/feedback values are writable; the point list does not describe how the drive uses them.

Network setup

  • The point list applies to drive firmware 3.90 and above.

Watch out for

  • Binary values prefixed "W:" are warning bits and "A:" are alarm bits.
  • Many rows in the list are Reserved or NA and are not included here.

Questions about the Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102)

How many BACnet points does the Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102) have?

This list has 297 BACnet objects: 7 analog inputs, 7 analog outputs, 50 analog values, 16 binary inputs, 31 binary outputs, 185 binary values, 1 multi-state output. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102) BACnet points are writable?

81 of the 297 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102) BACnet documentation?

The source is BacNET Points Firmware 3.90 and above (MCA109 FC-102 Advanced BACnet Option / FC-102 Embedded BACnet) (BGBACNET), revision December 2019, published free by Bell & Gossett at https://prod.xylem.com/siteassets/brand/bell-amp-gossett/resources/manual/bgbacnet.pdf.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102)?

Start with BV 1 Start (Start command); AV 0 Reference (Speed / process reference from the BAS); BV 5 Reset (Fault reset); BV 10 Drive Ready (Drive ready); BV 16 Motor running (Motor running); BV 14 Trip (Drive tripped); BV 13 Bus control (Drive under bus control); AV 50 Frequency (Output frequency).

How do I enable BMS control of the Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102)?

BV 1 Start, BV 2 Coasting, BV 3 CW/CCW and BV 4 Jog are writable commands, and BV 13 shows whether the drive is under bus control. The point list does not explain how bus control is set up on the drive.

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the Bell & Gossett MCA 109 Advanced BACnet Option (FC-102)?

AV 2-4 Bus Feedback1-3 and the Ext. 1-3 setpoint/feedback values are writable; the point list does not describe how the drive uses them.
