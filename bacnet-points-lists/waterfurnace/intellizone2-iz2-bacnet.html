BMS integration notes: WaterFurnace IntelliZone2 (IZ2) Zoning System, Aurora BACnet

Integration in brief

Map the system points (emergency shutdown, alarm reset, mode, alarm value) once and then one zone block per active zone. Each zone takes a BAS occupancy signal and occupied heating/cooling setpoints, and reports its occupancy status and usable temperature. A zone temperature from the BAS needs BV 11x0 set to BAS and the value on AV 11x2.

Enable & remote start

BV 101 em_shutdown_c shuts the unit down and BV 105 load_shed_c sheds load from the network. Occupancy can come from the BAS per zone (BV 11x6, MV 11x0 or AV 11x9, depending on BV 1000) or from the internal schedule; BV 502 picks internal or external schedule.

Sensors & values from the BMS

For a BAS zone temperature, set BV 11x0 (temp_selector) to BAS and write AV 11x2 (zone_temp_ovrd). AV 504, AV 506 and AV 508 accept BAS space humidity, CO2 and VOC values, and AV 606 a BAS outdoor air temperature (BV 1017 picks the OAT source).

Watch out for

  • Many points are flagged 'do not map to BAS'; leave them alone.
  • BV 318 gives the BAS write access to the ABC dip switch overrides; BV 398 takes it away.
  • Zone points repeat per zone: zone n uses instances 1n00-1n99 (zone 1 = 1100s).

Questions about the WaterFurnace IntelliZone2 (IZ2) Zoning System, Aurora BACnet

How many BACnet points does the WaterFurnace IntelliZone2 (IZ2) Zoning System, Aurora BACnet have?

This list has 546 BACnet objects: 354 analog values, 152 binary values, 40 multi-state values. The exact set on a given unit depends on its firmware, installed options and configuration.

Which WaterFurnace IntelliZone2 (IZ2) Zoning System, Aurora BACnet BACnet points are writable?

403 of the 546 objects accept a write. Points marked Command have a priority array: a write holds until it is released, so release it back to automatic when you are done.

Where is the official WaterFurnace IntelliZone2 (IZ2) Zoning System, Aurora BACnet BACnet documentation?

The source is IZ2 BACnet Point List, revision 2021-06-16, published free by WaterFurnace at https://www.waterfurnace.com/literature/intellizone/IZ2%20BACnet%20Point%20List.xlsx.

What are the minimum BACnet points to integrate the WaterFurnace IntelliZone2 (IZ2) Zoning System, Aurora BACnet?

Start with BV 101 em_shutdown_c (Emergency shutdown command); BV 1106 occ_man_cmd_bv_z1_c (Zone 1 occupancy from the BAS); AV 1111 occ_clg_setpt_z1_c (Zone 1 occupied cooling setpoint); AV 1112 occ_htg_setpt_z1_c (Zone 1 occupied heating setpoint); BV 130 alarm_reset_c (Alarm reset); MV 100 mode_msv (Unit operating mode); AV 1110 eff_occ_st_z1 (Zone 1 effective occupancy status); AV 1100 zone_temp_z1 (Zone 1 temperature in use); BV 141 alarm_hdw_stat_a (ABC alarm output status); AV 119 alarm_enum (Combined system alarm code).

How do I enable BMS control of the WaterFurnace IntelliZone2 (IZ2) Zoning System, Aurora BACnet?

BV 101 em_shutdown_c shuts the unit down and BV 105 load_shed_c sheds load from the network. Occupancy can come from the BAS per zone (BV 11x6, MV 11x0 or AV 11x9, depending on BV 1000) or from the internal schedule; BV 502 picks internal or external schedule.

Can the BMS supply sensor values to the WaterFurnace IntelliZone2 (IZ2) Zoning System, Aurora BACnet?

For a BAS zone temperature, set BV 11x0 (temp_selector) to BAS and write AV 11x2 (zone_temp_ovrd). AV 504, AV 506 and AV 508 accept BAS space humidity, CO2 and VOC values, and AV 606 a BAS outdoor air temperature (BV 1017 picks the OAT source).

Scan the network with a BACnet IP browser such as Easy BACnet, open the device and compare its object list with this table. Anything missing is usually an option that isn't installed or a point that is disabled in the controller's configuration.
