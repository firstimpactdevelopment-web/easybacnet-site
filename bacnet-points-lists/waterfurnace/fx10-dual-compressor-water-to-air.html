BMS integration notes: WaterFurnace Dual Compressor Water-to-Air Heat Pump (FX10)

Integration in brief

The BAS can run this heat pump in two ways: command occupancy (MO1) and let the FX10 hold its occupied/unoccupied setpoints, or act as the thermostat and send fan, Y1, Y2 and O calls directly (MO2-MO5). Space temperature can be overridden from the network on AO2, and MO6 forces an emergency shutdown. Keep frequent writes on the volatile AO/MO points, not the flash-backed AV/MV setpoints.

Enable & remote start

The sheet has no separate BAS-enable point. MO6 Emergency Override puts the unit in shutdown (5) or back to auto (1). MO2-MO5 let the BAS send fan, compressor and reversing-valve calls directly.

Sensors & values from the BMS

AO2 Space Temp Ovrd overrides the space temperature reading from the network. It is a volatile output, so it reverts to uncommanded after a power cycle. The sheet does not mention a network outdoor-air input.

Watch out for

  • AV/MV points are stored in flash (about 2,000,000 writes); write them only manually or by schedule, never from automatic reset logic.
  • Output points (AO/MO) are volatile and go back to uncommanded after a power cycle, so rewrite them after power is restored.
  • If the BAS shows 254 as uncommanded instead of 255, every multistate value is one lower (0=Off, 1=On).
  • MO8 (BO5 electric heat) is interlocked with BI4. The sheet says it needs MV2 Electric Heat Mode set to EmgHeat, which seems to conflict with MV2's own description, so confirm on site.
  • Alarm Reset needs a write of 2 followed by 1.

Integration notes

  • Output-type points are volatile and fall back to uncommanded after a power cycle; unlimited writes.
  • Value-type points (AV/MV) are stored in flash (about 2,000,000 write life); never write them from automatic reset logic.
  • Multistate numbers assume the BAS shows 255 as uncommanded; if yours shows 254, every state number is one lower (0=Off, 1=On).
  • No engineering units are printed in the source; temperatures are presumably degrees F.
  • The sheet does not state the BACnet transport.
